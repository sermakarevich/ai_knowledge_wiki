# The Impact of LLM-Assistants on Software Developer Productivity: A Systematic Review and Mapping Study

This folder synthesizes Mohamed et al.'s systematic review and mapping of 39 peer-reviewed studies (January 2014–December 2024) on LLM-assistants and developer productivity. The headline: assistants reliably accelerate well-scoped work but carry risks of over-reliance, flow disruption, and weakened collaboration, with code-quality effects unresolved.
