# What Is Graph Engineering?

This is a ~9-minute YouTube explainer by KGP Talkie (Lakshmikanth) that walks through the "agentic-AI technique stack" — prompt, context, harness, loop, and graph engineering — and lands on one central claim: **graph engineering is not a new technique**. It is what you get when you coordinate multiple loop-engineering solutions (agent-plus-evaluator pairs) together as nodes in a graph, something frameworks like LangGraph, Google ADK, and Microsoft AutoGen have been doing for years under other names. The video also draws a sharp line between graph engineering and GraphRAG, and closes with a cost-based decision rule for when the added complexity is actually worth it.
