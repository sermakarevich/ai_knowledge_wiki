# Graph Engineering Guide (2026)

A short AI Builder Club guide that decodes the term "graph engineering" — the label that trended on X in mid-July 2026 for wiring several specialized agents into a graph of nodes, edges, and shared state. Its central claim is double-edged: most tasks don't need a graph and should stay a single loop, but when the work genuinely exceeds one loop, splitting it into coordinated nodes is a real, defensible design step — regardless of whether the label itself is hype.
