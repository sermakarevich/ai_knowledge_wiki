# Connections

- [[ai_papers/graph_rag/ArxivSevenFailurePointsRAG/summary|Seven Failure Points in RAG]] — that paper catalogues *where* RAG pipelines fail from three production deployments (missing content, missed top-ranked docs, not extracted, etc.); ARES gives the complementary *measurement* layer — a way to statistically score whether a system exhibits those failures (via context relevance / answer faithfulness / answer relevance) rather than diagnosing them qualitatively.
- [[ai_papers/graph_rag/ArxivRAGvsGraphRAG/summary|RAG vs GraphRAG]] — that paper's controlled benchmark compares flat RAG against GraphRAG variants using standard retrieval metrics (F1, accuracy); ARES is the evaluation-methodology layer that could underlie such comparisons — its judge-plus-PPI approach is a candidate way to score GraphRAG's generation quality with statistical confidence rather than a single aggregate accuracy number.
- [[ai_papers/graph_rag/ArxivGraphRAGBench/summary|GraphRAGBench]] — a controlled, human-authored golden dataset for scoring GraphRAG's multi-hop reasoning; contrasts with ARES's approach of *generating* its own synthetic evaluation data rather than relying on a pre-built golden set — worth reading together as two different answers to "where does RAG/GraphRAG evaluation ground truth come from."
- [[ai_papers/graph_rag/ArxivGraphRAGUnderFire/summary|GraphRAG under Fire]] — studies how GraphRAG's shared-relation structure can be poisoned to corrupt many queries at once; ARES's answer-faithfulness judge is exactly the kind of automated hallucination detector that could, in principle, help surface such poisoning's downstream effect on generated answers, though neither paper tests that combination directly.
- [[papers/ArxivGraphRAGLinkedInCustomerService/summary|LinkedIn Customer-Service GraphRAG]] — a production KG-RAG case study reporting live A/B metrics (77.6% MRR, 28.6% faster resolution); ARES is the kind of offline, statistically-grounded evaluation framework that could have validated that system's retrieval/generation quality *before* the live A/B, rather than relying solely on the eventual business-metric outcome.

Both this paper and the entries above are part of the GraphRAG top-10 reading list at [[rag_and_retrieval/GraphRAGTop10Materials/index|GraphRAG Top 10 Materials]], where ARES is grouped (entry #9) with RAGBench and the RAGAS/TruLens/DeepEval evaluation-tooling landscape — that list's own critical note applies here too: none of these evaluation frameworks, including ARES, ship graph-native metrics (entity-linking correctness, path/traversal quality, community-summary faithfulness), so anyone evaluating GraphRAG specifically would need to extend ARES's three-axis rubric rather than apply it unmodified.
