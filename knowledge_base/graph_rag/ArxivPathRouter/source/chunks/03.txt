4
Experiments
We evaluate PATHROUTER to answer the following
research questions: RQ1: Does PATHROUTER im-
prove answer accuracy and evidence quality over
existing baselines? (§4.2) RQ2: Which compo-
nents drive path faithfulness, and how does rout-
ing change trajectory-level behavior? (§4.3, §4.4)
RQ3: How does teacher KL interact with model ca-
pacity, and do the learned retrieval strategies trans-
fer across datasets? (§4.5, §4.6)
4.1
Experimental Setup
Datasets. We follow the evaluation protocol of
Graph-R1 (Luo et al., 2025a) and use six QA bench-
marks: three multi-hop datasets (HotpotQA (Yang
et al., 2018), 2WikiMultiHopQA (Ho et al., 2020),
and MuSiQue (Trivedi et al., 2022)) that pro-
vide gold supporting facts enabling path faith-
fulness evaluation, and three single-hop datasets
(NQ (Kwiatkowski et al., 2019), PopQA (Mallen
et al., 2023), and TriviaQA (Joshi et al., 2017)) for
broader coverage.
Baselines.
Following Graph-R1 (Luo et al.,
2025a), we compare the following methods:
GPT-4o-mini-based methods as references:
GraphRAG (Edge et al., 2025), LightRAG (Guo
et al., 2025), PathRAG (Chen et al., 2025), Hip-
poRAG2 (Gutiérrez et al., 2025), and Hyper-
GraphRAG (Luo et al., 2025b).
Non-retrieval approaches: NaiveGeneration
(direct LLM), SFT (Zheng et al., 2024) (supervised
fine-tuning), and R1 (Shao et al., 2024) (GRPO
without retrieval).
Chunk-based retrieval: StandardRAG (Lewis
et al., 2020), Search-R1 (Jin et al., 2025), and R1-
Searcher (Song et al., 2025).
Graph-based agentic retrieval: Graph-R1 (Luo
et al., 2025a) (our primary baseline).
Evaluation Metrics. We report token-level F1,
Exact Match (EM), and GPT-4o-mini evaluation
(G-E) for answer quality, along with Supporting
Fact F1 (SF-F1) and Unsupported Answer Rate
(UAR, ↓) for path faithfulness. Formal definitions
are in Appendix A.
4.2
Main Results
Table 1 reports results across six datasets and
three model sizes, revealing two consistent patterns.
PATHROUTER consistently outperforms Graph-R1
across all model scales, improving 7B average F1
from 57.82 to 62.74, EM from 48.57 to 53.26, and
G-E from 76.23 to 78.72. The agreement across lex-
ical, exact-match, and LLM-based metrics suggests
the gains reflect more reliable answer production
rather than surface-form effects. Improvements are
