**Figure 3 – Routing‑category trajectory distributions (Qwen2.5‑7B‑Instruct).**

**What it shows.** A 2×3 grid of stacked‑bar plots, one per benchmark (HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, MuSiQue, NQ, PopQA, TriviaQA). Within each benchmark, three horizontal bars compare configurations: **Base** (no router), **GRPO** (RL baseline), and **Ours** (PathRouter). Each bar is partitioned into four mutually exclusive routing/trajectory categories defined by two binary signals — correctness (C) and faithfulness (P): **Faithful** (C↑,P↑, green), **Shortcut** (C↑,P↓, orange), **Evidence** (C↓,P↑, blue), and **Failure** (C↓,P↓, gray). Saturated colors mark the *Ours* row; lighter tints mark Base/GRPO.

**Axes / encoding.** The x‑axis is percentage share (0–100); because the categories are a partition, every bar sums to 100%. The y‑axis is the categorical (configuration × dataset) grouping. No quantitative y‑scale — the comparison is read off the segment widths.

**Trends.** Across all six datasets the *Ours* row consistently reallocates mass toward the two "good" regions — **Faithful** and **Evidence** — at the expense of the two "bad" regions — **Failure** and **Shortcut**. The shift is most pronounced where Base/GRPO fail heavily: e.g., on MuSiQue and NQ the gray *Failure* share drops sharply (roughly from ~40%/~35% down to ~25–30%), while on 2Wiki the *Evidence* share grows and *Shortcut* shrinks. On the easier single‑hop sets (NQ, PopQA, TriviaQA) the *Ours* row shows a clear increase in *Faithful* and a decrease in *Shortcut* (confident‑but‑unfaithful answers). GRPO is intermediate and often only marginally better than Base, with the *Failure* mass largely unchanged.

**Takeaway.** Introducing the router (PathRouter) does not just raise accuracy — it changes the *distribution* of trajectories: it suppresses both outright failures and the high‑confidence shortcut regime, and converts that mass into correct‑and‑faithful (Faithful) or evidence‑grounded (Evidence) behavior. In other words, the method improves the correct‑*and*‑faithful quadrant specifically, rather than trading faithfulness for accuracy, and it outperforms plain GRPO on this routing‑category decomposition.
