**What the figure shows.** This is a method/schematic diagram (not a data plot) for a graph‑conditioned, multi‑turn reasoning‑and‑retrieval training pipeline. It has three logical blocks.

1. **Left – "Graph-conditioned Multi-turn Rollouts."** A knowledge graph $G_g=(V,E)$ and a question $Q$ ("capital of Chopin's birth country?") condition the generation of $K$ sampled multi‑turn trajectories $\tau_1\ldots\tau_K$. Each trajectory is a sequence over turns $1\ldots T$ of typed tokens: *Think* (blue), *Retrieve Query* (orange), *Observation* (green), and a terminal *Answer* (star; starred‑with‑cross = wrong, blank = no answer). A *Gold Evidence* source $S_g$ and a *Frozen Teacher* provide supervision for the selected trajectory.

2. **Right – "Route by Correctness and Evidence Overlap."** A 2×2 decision matrix with categorical axes: rows = answer correctness $C_t$ (correct $=1$ vs wrong $=0$); columns = evidence overlap $P_t$ relative to a threshold $\theta_p$ (low $P_t<\theta_p$ vs high $P_t\ge\theta_p$). The four quadrants label the four outcome regimes: *Faithful Success* (C↑ P↑), *Shortcut Failure* (C↑ P↓, correct without real evidence), *Evidence Retrieved* (C↓ P↑, good evidence but wrong answer), and *Joint Failure* (C↓ P↓). This routes each trajectory.

3. **Bottom – Training signals.** A selected trajectory is shown with a **KL mask**: purple cells = tokens included for KL (the *Think* and *Retrieve‑Query* tokens), white cells = excluded (observations and answer). Two loss paths feed the **Student Model**: *RL on Trajectory* and *KL on Reasoning and Search‑query Tokens*.

**Axes and trends.** There are no numeric axes or empirical trends; the only "axes" are the categorical correctness × overlap matrix in the right panel. Arrows denote data/selection flow (rollouts → routing → masked trajectory → student), not quantitative relationships.

**Takeaway.** The framework generates graph‑conditioned multi‑turn search/reasoning rollouts, classifies each trajectory by *whether it is correct* and *whether it actually used the graph evidence* (to catch shortcut solutions), and then trains a student model with a hybrid objective: trajectory‑level RL plus **selective KL distillation applied only to reasoning and retrieval‑query tokens** (observations masked out), using a frozen teacher and gold evidence. In short, it couples retrieval‑aware rollout selection with token‑masked distillation to teach faithful, evidence‑grounded multi‑turn reasoning.
