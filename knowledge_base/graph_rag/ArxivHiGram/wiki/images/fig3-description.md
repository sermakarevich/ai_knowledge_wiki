**Figure 3 — Hyperparameter sensitivity on LoCoMo.** The figure contains two line plots that test how HiGram's performance reacts to its two main retrieval/search hyperparameters: (a) varying *K_g*, the number of retrieved MicroGraphs (x‑axis ≈ 8, 12, 16), and (b) varying *K_p*, the number of enumerated evidence paths (x‑axis ≈ 16, 24, 32). The y‑axis in both panels is a score axis running roughly from 40 to 90. Three metrics are tracked in each panel: **Avg. LLM‑J** (green dashed line), **Avg. F1** (blue solid line), and **Avg. BLEU** (brown dashed line).

**Trends.** In both subplots all three metrics rise monotonically but only gradually as the corresponding hyperparameter increases, then begin to flatten. LLM‑J is consistently the highest curve (roughly the low‑80s to mid‑80s), while F1 and BLEU sit lower (roughly the mid‑40s to mid‑50s) and track each other closely; BLEU in panel (b) is nearly flat between the last two settings. Overall the curves are gently increasing and saturating rather than sharply peaked.

**Takeaway.** HiGram's performance is stable and insensitive to these hyperparameters: larger *K_g* helps modestly by providing more relevant memory regions (gains then saturate), and varying *K_p* produces only limited change, indicating the path‑level scoring still finds relevant evidence across search budgets. In short, the system does not require careful tuning of *K_g* or *K_p*.
