**Figure 3 — HippoRAG Pipeline Example (Question and Annotations).**

*What it shows.* This is not a quantitative plot; it is a qualitative, three‑part textual schematic illustrating a single end‑to‑end example from the MuSiQue "path‑following" dataset, used to demonstrate the HippoRAG indexing/retrieval pipeline. There are therefore no axes, scales, or trends to report. The three panels are: (1) **Question & Answer** — Q: "In which district was Alhandra born?" A: "Lisbon"; (2) **Supporting Passages** — two excerpts that jointly answer the question, the *Alhandra (footballer)* entry (born in Vila Franca de Xira, Lisbon) and the *Vila Franca de Xira* entry (a municipality in the **Lisbon District**, pop. ≈ 1.4×10⁵, area ≈ 3.2×10² km²); and (3) **Distractor Passages (Excerpts)** — three unrelated excerpts (*Chirakkalkulam* in Kannur District, Kerala, India; the *Frank T. and Polly Lewis House* in the Portage Street Historic District, Wisconsin, USA; and a *Birth certificate* entry mentioning "capital district").

*Takeaway.* The example encodes a multi‑hop reasoning chain (Alhandra → birthplace Vila Franca de Xira → its district, Lisbon) that requires **two** supporting passages to resolve, while the distractors are constructed to share the surface keyword "district" from the question. The design point is that a retrieval pipeline must link the two relevant passages and suppress the lexically similar but irrelevant distractors — i.e., disambiguate by entity/relationship rather than by the query term alone.
