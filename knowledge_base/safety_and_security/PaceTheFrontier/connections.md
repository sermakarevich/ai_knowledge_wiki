# Connections

- [[research/SituationalAwareness/summary|Situational Awareness: The Decade Ahead]] — Same-thesis-different-author: Aschenbrenner's 2024 essay argues recursive self-improvement will produce superintelligence within years and that democracies must out-race authoritarian states while managing the risk, using the same "few years of dangerous acceleration, democratic lead must be defended" framing and even the same SALT-treaty arms-control analogy that Amodei uses here — Pace the Frontier is the operational follow-up (concrete evaluator/checkpoint/treaty ladder) to Situational Awareness's alarm.
- [[research/ClaudeSonnet5SystemCard/summary|System Card: Claude Sonnet 5]] — Same-org-applied-instance: the System Card is Anthropic's actual Responsible Scaling Policy evaluation in practice (capability thresholds like CB-2 and automated-AI-R&D, self-conducted via automated evaluations) — it is the present-day, self-audited version of exactly the capability-based checkpoint regime this essay argues should instead be verified by independent embedded evaluators with publish rights.
- [[research/EconomicScenariosForTransformativeAI/summary|Economic Scenarios for Transformative AI]] — Same-institution-different-lens: an Anthropic Institute framework modeling GDP, labor share, and unemployment paths under modest/substantial/extreme AI capability scenarios (2026-2030) — the economic upside and disruption risk this essay cites as reasons to pace (rather than halt) capability growth are exactly the outcomes that paper tries to quantify.
