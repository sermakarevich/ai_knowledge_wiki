# We Must Pace the Frontier

Dario Amodei's essay argues that AI capability growth — driven by recursive self-improvement — is now accelerating faster than the safety work needed to control it, so frontier labs and governments must deliberately pace the *rate* of capability advancement rather than only racing to do safety work alongside full-speed progress. It was worth ingesting because it is a frontier lab's CEO publicly committing his own company first to a concrete, falsifiable mechanism (independently verifiable embedded evaluators with contractual publish rights) rather than making an abstract call for caution, and because it lays out an escalation ladder — unilateral, then democratic-industry, then global — that connects AI-safety rhetoric to specific policy and treaty mechanisms.
