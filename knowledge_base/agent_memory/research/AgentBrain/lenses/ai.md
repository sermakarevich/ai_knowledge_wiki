# agent_memory -- for AI engineers working with agent memory and RAG

## What this means for you

- Pure vector search over flat chat history is the wrong baseline. Every recall substrate in this survey adds structure and fuses signals: BM25 + vector + graph with RRF or weighted fusion, plus cross-encoder reranking in the heaviest setup (Hindsight), budget caps (TencentDB), and scan-before-read layering so the agent judges relevance before loading full text (TencentDB L0→L3, OpenViking L0/L1/L2).
- Store distilled, inspectable artifacts — not transcripts. The dominant pattern is layered distillation (conversation → atoms → scenarios → persona/skills) and Markdown files as the inspectable substrate: session-committed memories (OpenViking), an agent-maintained Markdown skill wiki as the whole store (MemU), Obsidian `Brain/*.md` with git history and no daemon (OpenSecondBrain), plus Skills/CodeGraph assets and world/experience facts plus mental models (TencentDB, Hindsight).
- Scope and budget every recall call. Replace global vector pools with subtree-scoped `find`/`search`/`grep` over `viking://` (OpenViking), `bank_id` tenancy over every retain/recall/reflect call (Hindsight), and `private`/`team`/`restricted` visibility plus User/Role/Agent ACLs on versioned assets (TencentDB) — with token caps and tier-first scanning.
- Treat the knowledge graph as a companion provenance layer over Markdown, not a replacement. Files stay Obsidian-compatible Markdown; the graph (ledgers, `graph.json`, ontology, index) carries typed edges with `extracted`/`inferred`/`ambiguous` tags, contradiction flags, evidence spans, confidence, and asserting document. Retrieval fuses lexical + vector + graph proximity, with deterministic BM25/FTS fallback when models are unavailable.
- Gate every write; model staleness explicitly. New links land via inspected transaction bundles, `compile --approve` with `wiki/candidates/` staging and `lint --conflicts`, review-gated entity-resolution proposals with quarantine, or suggestion-only Canvas links needing accept/dismiss. Freshness is modelled as ledger review state plus dead-link/orphan lint, decay scoring (`freshness.defaultHalfLifeDays`), bi-temporal edges with contradiction invalidation and `as_of` point-in-time answers, or live Hot/Cold tiering from links plus recency.
- Keep capture local-first with provenance at ingest time. Lanes run from clipboard (10-second keep-gate popup, local SQLite), Telegram chat (agent-classified typed cards), subtitle-first platform ingest (`yt-dlp`, on-device transcription), resident Zotero indexes (BM25 ~15 ms), and folder-drop bulk builds (multimodal JSON-plus-markdown evidence bundles) — each preserving source URLs, entry IDs, digests, or file-and-page identity, and feeding maintained indexes (dedup/queue/retry, token-budgeted ranked search, nightly classification with MOC rebuild, validation gates that fail bad data).
- Close the loop as memory-fed skills, not passive Q&A. The shared idiom is skills-as-executable-markdown (`SKILL.md` as the artifact), brain-first read rules and hot-cache prompt injection, checkpointed durability (Goals/leases, thread checkpoints with exactly-once completion, `AC-n` evidence rows) instead of replay, delegated orchestration (lead synthesis over cheap worker fan-out), provider chains with failover/cooldown, and approval/RBAC gates with audit trails over outward effects.

## Decisions this informs

- **Retrieval architecture:** choose three-channel BM25 + vector + graph-proximity fusion with rerank and budget caps over single-method vector search; decide fusion weights (`search.hybrid_weight_graph`), RRF k (e.g. 60 attested), fan-out breadth (Hindsight's four-strategy vs. lightweight tiered scan), and deterministic fallback.
- **Representation:** choose layered distillation (L0 abstract → L1 overview → L2 full) and Markdown-first inspectability vs. relational/pgvector banks vs. virtual filesystem vs. team asset hub; decide what gets distilled (facts, skills, mental models, CodeGraph impact paths) rather than replayed.
- **Scoping and governance:** decide recall boundaries (subtree, bank, team, ACL/visibility) and write governance weight (transaction bundles, compile-approve staging, quarantine, suggestion-only verdicts); decide aliasing/dedup posture (frontmatter aliases, review-gated merges with no-fold rules, query-time synonyms, opt-in LLM keep/fold/drop).
- **Freshness and contradiction:** pick a staleness model — decay half-life, bi-temporal invalidation with `as_of` queries, Hot/Cold tiering, or lint-reported staleness — and decide how contradiction demotes or invalidates edges.
- **LLM placement and cost:** decide where inference runs — required on every retain/recall/reflect path (Hindsight), embedding-only service with agent-side judgment (MemU), keyless BM25 with doubly opt-in LLM compression (Agentmemory), file-only harnesses with zero inference, or routed providers with failover (ordered chain plus cooldown; local Ollama vs. keys vs. subscription/OAuth vs. Workers AI).
- **Capture and deployment footprint:** choose ingest mouths (clipboard, chat, subtitles, library, folder-drop) and keep-decisions (human-gated, agent-classified, build-validated); choose deployment shape — local desktop (OS credential store), single JAR/Docker/Electron over Postgres, user-owned Cloudflare Worker (D1 + Vectorize + Workers AI + KV), or zero-server markdown plugin — with cloud calls confined to opt-in transcription/enrichment edges.

## Risks and unknowns

- **Benchmark evidence is thin and vendor-reported.** LoCoMo 80–83% with 34–91% fewer tokens (OpenViking), LongMemEval SOTA with third-party reproduction (Hindsight), and 95.2% R@5 with 92% fewer tokens (Agentmemory) are snapshot/vendor claims; TencentDB, MemU, and OpenSecondBrain make no quantitative recall claims, and no action-harness source reports task-success, latency, or recall-precision figures for the action loop.
- **Engine internals are unverified.** Claims rest on README/config-level syntheses; Rust/C++ internals, index schemas, handler-level code, ranking formulas, and several provider/route details (exact model IDs, full REST tables) are out of scope or marked unverified, and several summaries flag truncated chunks or missing `src/` pages.
- **Local-first framing has exceptions.** OpenWiki's default-on Jina Reader/Google Translate calls weaken its local-first claim; Chubbyskills PDF import is text-layer only with no OCR, so image-only scans fail at ingest; transcription strategy varies (subtitle reuse, Deepgram cloud, local whisper/FunASR, Office/PDF renderers) with different cost/privacy trade-offs.
- **No agreement on store shape, LLM dependence, or orchestration weight.** Relational banks vs. virtual filesystems vs. vault files; LLM-everywhere vs. embedding-only vs. keyless BM25; heavyweight durable Team Runs vs. lightweight name-composed skills — these are design choices, not settled answers, and recall-machinery weight ranges from four-strategy fan-out with reranking to progressive tiered retrieval.
