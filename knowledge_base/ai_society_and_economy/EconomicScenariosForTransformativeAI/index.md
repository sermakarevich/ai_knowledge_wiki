# Economic Scenarios for Transformative AI

Anthropic Institute Working Paper No. 2026-02 (Korinek, Jones, Sacher, Cotter, McCrory, September 2026). A task-based economic framework that converts assumptions about AI capabilities (2026-2030) into comparable paths for GDP, growth, wages, the labor share, reallocation, and unemployment -- illustrated with modest, substantial, and extreme scenarios plus a representative US-adult survey. Worth ingesting as a structured way to compare AI futures rather than as a prediction.
