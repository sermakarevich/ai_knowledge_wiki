# AI's Hidden Debt Problem, Explained

Reports claim five AI hyperscalers — Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, and Oracle — carry over $1 trillion (elsewhere $3 trillion) in hidden off-balance-sheet borrowings for AI data centers, with OpenAI committing $600B+ by 2030. This video (The Plain Bagel, ~22min) checks the claim against each firm's own filings and splits the true off-balance-sheet total (>$2.6T) into four buckets: non-cancellable purchase commitments, funding/construction commitments, uncommenced leases, and financial guarantees — walking through Meta's Hyperion and Soapia special-purpose-vehicle (SPV) deals as worked examples of how guarantees quietly shift SPV bond debt onto a parent company. The verdict: only ~$100B across two firms is a true financial guarantee, so the "$1T+ hidden debt" alarm is partly overblown, but the underlying shift toward heavy, fixed AI infrastructure costs — ahead of proven AI revenue — is a real and growing risk.
