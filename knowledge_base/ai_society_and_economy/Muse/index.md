# Muse — Meta's Personal AI Agent

On Sept 8, 2026 Meta launched Muse, a personal AI agent that does tasks (email, travel, payments) across connected apps instead of just answering questions. It runs on Muse Secure VM (Virtual Machine, an isolated computer in the cloud) with a Sentinel permission gate, and press coverage (Reuters, Axios) adds pricing detail and reports of mixed internal tests. Worth ingesting as the highest-profile test of whether mainstream users will trust an agent with their accounts.
