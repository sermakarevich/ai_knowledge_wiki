# The AI economy: Interconnected

This folder distills the McKinsey Global Institute's September 2026 report arguing that AI's big questions — jobs, investment payoffs, regulation, company ROI — can only be answered by managing the whole interconnected system at multiple speeds. Capabilities sprint exponentially while power grids, chip supply, and organizational redesign lag linearly over years, so bottlenecks migrate and adoption that outruns governance invites backlash.
