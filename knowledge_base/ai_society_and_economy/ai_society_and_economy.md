# AI Society & Economy

Research on **AI's impact on work, learning, organizational design, and economic productivity** — covering skill formation, workforce restructuring, and AI-vs-human comparisons in professional domains.

## Papers

- [[81kEconomicsOfAI/summary]] — Anthropic survey of 81k Claude users finds job-threat anxiety scales with AI task exposure; scope expansion (48%) outpaces speed gains (40%), with a U-shaped anxiety-speedup curve.
- [[AIImpactsSkillFormation/summary]] — AI coding assistance speeds tasks slightly but causes 17% drop in post-task learning; debugging skills suffer most.
- [[AgenticAIvsHumanEconomists/summary]] — Agentic AI (Codex GPT-5.4) matches human economists on causal inference with less dispersion; AI reviewers rank AI submissions above human ones.
- [[FromAGIToASI/summary]] — Google DeepMind maps four pathways and six bottlenecks on the AGI-to-ASI roadmap, replacing speculation with a structured, falsifiable research agenda.
