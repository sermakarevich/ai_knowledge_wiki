# AI for Good: How Real People Are Using Artificial Intelligence to Fix Things That Matter

An advance reader's proof of Josh Tyrangiel's nonfiction book (Simon & Schuster), which argues that AI's most valuable real-world use is amplifying human judgment rather than replacing it. Rather than tech-industry executives, it profiles ordinary practitioners -- teachers, doctors, bureaucrats, parents -- solving specific problems across education, medicine, government, and human connection, treating both their successes and their failures as part of the honest record. Worth ingesting as a grounded, narrative counterpoint to the technical/research-heavy AI literature elsewhere in this KB.
