# There Will Be a Scientific Theory of Deep Learning -- Deep Dive

The paper is a position/synthesis paper organized around the idea that a unified scientific theory of deep learning -- which the authors call **learning mechanics** -- is emerging. Its argument rests on five lines of evidence (Sec 2.1-2.5), connections to adjacent fields (Sec 3), responses to skepticism (Sec 4), open research directions (Sec 5), and an onboarding chapter for newcomers (Sec 6). The deep-dive sections below mirror this structure.
