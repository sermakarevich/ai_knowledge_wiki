# Data pipeline and training procedure
**In one sentence:** AVTR-1 trains on 926 retained hours of unscripted dyadic conversation distilled from 34,219 candidate videos through annotation, scene/face filtering, audiovisual speaker separation, and motion-outlier rejection, optimizing a regional flow-matching loss plus smoothness, cosine, and VAD-gate terms under a teacher-forcing rollout curriculum and multi-condition guidance dropout with the Adan optimizer.
## Key points
- Source selection: 34,219 candidate videos → 1,472 retained by human annotators (1,370.13 hours), each with at least one two-visible-participant conversational segment, faces frontal/slight-angle and unoccluded; short-form, edited excerpts, and uncertain cases excluded.
- Scene detection: PySceneDetect split, first 90 s discarded, scenes under 5 s dropped; every 25th frame face-checked (160 px minimum at 1080p, YOLO person-detector fallback when face count ≠ 2); only ≥5 s all-pass segments kept.
- Face filtering: fixed left/right participant regions, whole-body pose estimator per region, 68 facial landmarks inside the matching half with mean confidence above 0.3, ≥5 s sequences; produces the frame-level landmarks later stages consume.
- Speaker separation: mono 16 kHz audio split with ClearerVoice-Studio's pretrained audiovisual target-speaker extractor using cropped lip videos to assign outputs to sides; one participant randomly becomes the generation target (self audio) and the other becomes other audio.
- Audio features: both separated waveforms encoded once with the original HuBERT encoder before training (full-length context, sampling windows without re-encoding); concatenated dual 1024-dim streams stored per segment (2048-dim per frame).
- Motion extraction: landmark-defined crops smoothed over 25 frames, resized to 256×256, left/right crops batched through the LivePortrait motion extractor; full output stored per track; every stage caches intermediates so CPU/GPU workloads schedule separately and components revise without rerunning earlier steps.
- Window filtering: per-coordinate scaled first/second/third finite differences of rotation and expression, averaged, smoothed over 21 frames; frames scoring above 0.15 (plus 5-frame margins) are outliers; only 25-frame clean windows kept; segments with no surviving windows on either track are discarded.
- Loss: regional flow-matching MSE over rotation/brow/eyes/mouth with logit-normal timestep shared per chunk (LCFM), regional cosine-direction loss (Lcos), smoothness regularizer over expression/rotation/reconstructed-3D-keypoint differences up to third order with λexp = 0.1, λrot = λ3D = 100 (Lreg, history-prepended across chunk boundaries), and BCE audio-gate supervision on confident single-speaker frames with λVAD = 0.01; Ltotal is their sum.
- Noise design: progressive (autoregressive, cross-chunk-correlated) noise trajectories plus per-region immiscible reassignment within each batch to shorten noise-to-target distance.
- Autoregressive rollout: each 25-frame target split into five chunks; one-step clean estimates update the rolling 75-frame history under a curriculum that starts fully teacher-forced and moves the ground-truth→estimate boundary one chunk earlier per epoch from the final chunk; estimates carry a learned timestep embedding so the decoder distinguishes them from ground truth (t = 1).
- Guidance dropout: self/other/reference kept together 45%, all dropped 15%, one-or-two retained otherwise; past-motion history masked separately (retained 75%); at inference the past-only prediction is the baseline with per-region guidance residuals per condition.
- Optimization: Adan with three weight-decay groups (0.05 most matrices, 0.01 AdaLN/timestep/reference projections, 0 for 1-D params/biases/gates/quality embeddings); linear warm-up over 5,000 steps to 3×10⁻⁴, 200,000-step cosine decay to 10⁻⁵; EMA decay 0.995 used for the released checkpoint; 200,000 steps on one NVIDIA GH200 in ~35 hours, float32 with TF32 matmuls.
