**In one sentence:** This closing chunk contains bibliography entries [31]–[40] on lip reading, audio-visual enhancement, SNN robustness, and power measurement plus Akida documentation, followed by author biographies and the paper's closing page footers.

## Author biographies and closing footers
**Covers:** Biography block through pp. 12–13 footers

- LEONIDAS DELIMPASIS received the five-year (M.Eng. equivalent) degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the National Technical University of Athens, Greece, in 2025, where his diploma thesis focused on Neuro-Symbolic AI for Visual Question Answering; contributed to several European research projects; was an ML Researcher at Tech Hive Labs; currently a Machine Learning Engineer at Plaixus and serves under the Hellenic National Defence General Staff, Athens, Greece, with interests in interpretable machine learning, end-to-end AI development/deployment, and AI in medical, industrial, and security domains.
- MICHAIL KARAMOUSADAKIS received the five-year (M.Sc. equivalent) degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from NTUA in 2019 and M.Sc. in Rehabilitation Engineering from NKUA in 2023; interests include AI, machine learning, edge computing, DevOps/MLOps, and robotics; co-authored works on edge AI systems, cyber-physical security, and data-driven intelligent platforms.
- PANAGIOTA MORAITI received the five-year (M.Eng. equivalent) degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Democritus University of Thrace in 2024 with a thesis on Continual Test-Time Adaptation in autonomous driving; Computer Vision Engineer at Tech Hive Labs; interests include computer vision, deep learning, robotics; co-authored publications on Domain Adaptation, Continual Learning, and Automated Quality Control.
- ANTONIS PORICHIS received the Diploma in Electrical and Computer Engineering from NTUA in 2012 and Ph.D. in Robotics and AI from the University of Essex in 2024; Research Officer at the University of Essex; interests include machine learning, computer vision, robotic manipulation, and data-efficient learning (imitation learning, learning from demonstration).
- PANOS CHATZAKOS received Diploma, M.Sc., and Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from NTUA; Director of the Essex Artificial Intelligence Innovation Centre (University of Essex / TWI Ltd.); led multidisciplinary AI/robotics teams and large-scale research and innovation projects across Europe.
