# StepAudio 3 Realtime Technical Report

**Paper:** [StepAudio 3 Realtime Technical Report (StepFun-Audio Team, 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14005)

## Human Readable TL;DR

StepAudio 3 Realtime is like a skilled dinner-party host who can listen, talk, think, and fetch things from the kitchen all at the same time without dropping the conversation. Like a simultaneous interpreter who starts speaking before the speaker has finished, it begins answering out loud while it is still thinking through the hard parts in the background, so complex questions get careful reasoning without awkward pauses. And like a good host who can tell the difference between a polite "uh-huh," a real interruption, and chatter meant for someone else, it manages pauses, backchannels, interruptions, and background noise to keep the conversational floor flowing naturally.

## TL;DR

StepAudio 3 Realtime is an audio-language foundation model organized around a continuous listen-converse-think-act loop that couples Deep Perception, Seamless Duplex floor management, Think-While-Speaking reasoning, and a streaming Voice Agent for asynchronous tool use. Built as a full-duplex mixture-of-experts system with a Qwen3-Omni Audio Transformer encoder plus adapter and LLM decoder, it is pretrained in three stages at 32K length over 1.2T tokens and midtrained to 128K context for long histories and tool results, with StepAudio 3 ASR Max diverging only at supervised fine-tuning for transcription. The realtime system scores 73.0 macro average on StepAudioChat in reasoning mode (70.4 in interactive realtime mode), 90.6 on MMSU, 98.9 Overall on the Artificial Analysis Full-Duplex Bench, and 56.0% macro task-success on τ-Voice, while ASR Max reports error rates of 1.18 on LibriSpeech clean, 4.35 on WenetSpeech meeting, and 0.49 on AISHELL-1.

---

## Problem & Motivation

Spoken interaction demands two things that normally conflict: deep deliberation about complex, evolving requests and immediate, fluid turn-taking with natural pauses, backchannels, and interruptions. A pause may precede a complete request, overlapping speech may be a mere acknowledgment or a substantive correction, and tool work such as retrieval or multi-step backend tasks can outlast the exchange that started it, all while the user keeps talking. Prior audio-language work combined acoustic representations with LLM knowledge, added direct speech generation, and introduced streaming and full-duplex overlap, but coordinating perception, reasoning, and action as conversation unfolds remained the gap. StepAudio 3 Realtime therefore frames the problem as a concurrent listen-converse-think-act loop in which acoustic and linguistic evidence, dialogue history, the current speaking turn, reasoning progress, and tool-execution status jointly decide whether to keep listening, keep speaking, reason further, or act externally, so that deliberation and long-running tasks proceed alongside rather than blocking the dialogue.

## Main Original Ideas

1. **Think-While-Speaking with Formulation and Articulation Brains** — building on the Mind-Paced Speaking two-process design, two concurrent calls to the same audio model act as a Formulation Brain generating a private reasoning trace and an Articulation Brain producing short spoken segments conditioned on reasoning available so far and speech already delivered, with playback-aware scheduling, Speak-First by default and Think-First prefix-waiting as the option, plus a final continuation that can supplement or correct answers begun from incomplete reasoning.

2. **Adaptive Thinking for per-turn deliberation routing** — different turns warrant different deliberation budgets, so supervision is built at the assistant-turn level by comparing original reasoning trajectories against no-think regenerations from a fixed probe model under blind judging, then applying per-domain no-think budgets with stratified retention so reasoning-intensive capabilities are not stripped, letting routine turns answer immediately while complex ones keep explicit reasoning.

3. **Seamless Duplex floor management over dual interleaved streams** — user and model audio form synchronized full-duplex streams organized into 320 ms blocks each followed by a state or text token, and the system combines acoustic timing with semantic completeness and dialogue history to distinguish within-turn pauses from turn endings, brief backchannel acknowledgments from substantive interruptions, and directed speech from background chatter, trained with streaming ASR, VAD, and utterance-completeness supervision on over 10,000 hours of synthetic full-duplex data.

4. **Deep Perception with a specialized ASR Max branch** — perception retains both what the user says and how it is said, covering lexical content, paralinguistics, acoustic events, speaker and temporal structure, music, and audio-grounded reasoning, while ASR Max shares pretraining and midtraining with the realtime model and diverges only at SFT with 32K-packed sequences, frozen encoder, SpecAugment-style masking, ROVER fusion of multi-system hypotheses, LLM punctuation restoration, and long-tail terminology augmentation, complemented by a five-stage audio-understanding SFT pipeline with multi-model agreement and case-value filtering.

5. **Streaming Voice Agent for asynchronous tool execution** — requests are routed among direct responses for stable knowledge, lightweight tools such as weather and web search for up-to-date public information, and asynchronous backend execution for private-context, multi-step, or beyond-turn work, with trained discipline to clarify missing information and obtain confirmation before consequential actions, associate in-flight user input with the ongoing task versus unrelated dialogue, and fold returned evidence back into conversational context in a ReAct-style interleaving supported by targeted dialogues, filtered multi-step trajectories, and negative examples against gratuitous invocation.

6. **MTP acceleration, teacher merging, and StepAudioChat evaluation** — remaining private reasoning is accelerated with MTP3 multi-token prediction under Medusa-style typical acceptance with entropy-adaptive thresholds and a 1.05 repetition penalty on thinking only, four compatible teachers from a common base are merged in parameter space with 3:1:1:1 weighting to balance audio, text, and dialogue strengths without added components or routing, and the closed text-based StepAudioChat benchmark with hierarchical capability taxonomy, naturalistic valid/flawed response pairs, calibrated difficulty, and factorized multi-turn dialogue data isolates text-level conversational intelligence from prosody and timing.

## Key Findings

StepAudio 3 ASR Max leads all three standard ASR families with best results on both LibriSpeech subsets and AISHELL-1 and leads all four ContextASR-Bench subsets in the Contextless setting with macro-average error rates of 5.67% on English and 1.23% on Mandarin versus 6.60% and 1.69% for the next best system, confirming strong transcription including long-tail terminology, though these results characterize the specialized ASR model rather than the realtime model. On audio understanding the realtime model leads four of eight benchmarks with its largest margins on MMSU at 90.6 and MMAR at 86.5 for gains of 7.0 and 4.8 points, also leads Step-Caption and MTalk-Bench, runs close on MMAU and WildSpeech, but trails substantially on AudioMultiChallenge by 17.7 points while Big Bench Audio is nearly saturated, and a quality-control ablation shows roughly 100K curated SFT examples beating roughly 2M random ones by about 11 points on MMSU and 10 points on MMAR. Conversationally the system ranks first on the Artificial Analysis full-duplex evaluation at 98.9 overall with 100.0 on turn taking and 99.0 on interruption handling, reaches 73.0 macro average on StepAudioChat in reasoning mode ahead of Doubao 2.0 Lite and DeepSeek-V4-Flash but behind Kimi K3 at 77.1, and retains 70.4 in interactive realtime mode to show deliberation alongside speech rather than shallow low-latency responses. Tool-grounded τ-Voice task completion reaches 56.0% macro success close to the best reported 56.5%, with the strongest telecom score at 70.2% and near-best airline at 60.0% but weaker retail at 37.7%, while general text leads HMMT 2026 Feb at 86.8 but trails on GPQA Diamond and MultiChallenge, MTP configurations deliver 1.49× to 2.05× wall-clock speedups while improving Reasoning and Memory but lowering Instruction Following, and Adaptive Thinking invokes reasoning at 51.5% to 82.0% across categories with its Reasoning think rate of only 59.5% despite that category gaining most from full thinking.

## Suggestions & Future Directions

The report itself flags multi-turn constraint following over natural audio and retail tool-use tasks as the clearest areas for improvement, alongside the AudioMultiChallenge deficit that points to maintaining and revising constraints across turns. Adaptive Thinking reduces how often explicit reasoning runs but with uneven effects on quality, and the category rates expose a selection-policy limitation in which reasoning is under-allocated to the turns that benefit most, motivating better allocation of deliberation budgets and continued work on when explicit reasoning actually changes answer quality. MTP findings caution that accepted draft tokens alone do not determine net efficiency since reasoning length and decoding costs also matter, that reported speedups should not be read as a controlled comparison of draft depth, and that strict verification on spoken output does not eliminate errors from incomplete private reasoning, suggesting further study of depth trade-offs and grounding of early speech in partial thought. The agentic results invite closing the retail gap through stronger confirmation discipline, evidence grounding, and long-horizon task tracking under interruptions, backchannels, and background noise, while the data ablations argue for prioritizing quality-controlled, high case-value, cross-model-consistent SFT data over raw volume in future audio-understanding and dialogue training.
