# The Ultimate Voice AI Tier List: Vapi, LiveKit, Retell, ElevenLabs, Telnyx, Pipecat

Piotr ranks Vapi, ElevenLabs, Pipecat, Retell, LiveKit, and Telnyx on a 0 (terrible) to 2 (perfect) scale, weighted for small, medium, and large businesses.
