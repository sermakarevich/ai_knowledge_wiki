# Pipeline, Persona/Script Generation, and Expressive Synthesis
**In one sentence:** DuplexDrama is built via a four-stage pipeline that generates consistent persona–scenario setups, writes dialogue scripts annotated with full-duplex, emotion, and sound-event tags, synthesizes them with IndexTTS2 using a 64-voice pool, and realizes script-placed background channels at FA-aligned positions.
## Key points
- The pipeline has four stages: (§2.1) persona and scenario generation, (§2.2) tagged script generation, (§2.3) IndexTTS2 expressive synthesis plus audio assembly, (§2.4) script-aware background/event injection, followed by a validator (§2.5) that discards low-quality dialogues.
- Stage 1 builds two seed pools with DeepSeek-v4.1-pro — a persona pool of 24 macro categories / 134 sub-types by social function and a topic pool of 46 macro categories / 404 sub-types by narrative scenario — then expands a drawn seed into two persona tuples plus one scenario specification under consistency constraints.
- Stage 2 uses Gemini-3.1-pro-preview to produce spoken-style dialogue annotated with three orthogonal tag families: full-duplex behavior tags, seven emotion tags, and environmental-noise / sound-event tags referencing the audio bank.
- Stage 3 synthesizes with IndexTTS2 for disentangled timbre–emotion control, using a 64-speaker pool spanning 13 core personas and 5 age buckets; most prompt audios are generated by MOSS-Audio with a small fraction curated from natural speech snippets.
- Per-utterance audio goes through forced alignment for word-level timestamps, then turn-order assembly into two parallel speaker tracks with silence inserted during the other speaker's turns, yielding two long-form waveforms.
- Full-duplex acoustics are realized jointly: `<user incomplete>` becomes a literal ". . . " rendered as an in-sentence pause, `<user interrupt>` fades the utterance out while the other turn is brought in (acoustic overlap), and `<user backchannel>` is time-aligned and overlaid while the other track keeps speaking (simultaneous speech).
- Stage 4 builds two background channels per dialogue (one per speech channel) from 7 scene categories / 600 long-duration noise clips and 52 categories / 2,203 short-duration event clips, inserting events at FA-aligned trigger positions with configurable-SNR loudness; only the input (HM)-side background channel is required for full-duplex model training.

## Full-duplex dialogue synthesis
**Covers:** §2.3

| Item | Value (verbatim/from chunk) |
|---|---|
| Synthesizer | IndexTTS2, supporting disentangled control of speaker timbre and emotion |
| Voice pool | 64 speakers spanning 13 core personas and 5 age buckets |
| Prompt audio source | Most generated by MOSS-Audio; small fraction curated from natural speech snippets |
| Conditioning | A speaker prompt audio and an emotion tag jointly condition IndexTTS2 at synthesis time |
| Alignment | Forced alignment (FA) on all utterances to obtain word-level timestamps |
| Assembly | Utterances assembled by turn order into two parallel tracks; each track carries only its own speaker with silence inserted during the other's turns, yielding two long-form speech waveforms |

Full-duplex acoustic realization (verbatim mechanisms):

> "`<user incomplete>` tags are replaced by literal '. . . ' inside sentence, which IndexTTS2 renders as in-sentence pauses during synthesis."

> "For an `<user interrupt>` tag, the corresponding utterance fades out briefly while the other turn is brought in, producing acoustic overlap."

> "For an `<user backchannel>` tag, the corresponding utterance is time-aligned and overlaid onto the other track which continues speaking, yielding simultaneous speech."

## Background channel realization (as present in this chunk)
**Covers:** §2.4 (tag placement + banks + mixing, per chunk text)

| Item | Value (verbatim/from chunk) |
|---|---|
| Tag placement | Environmental noise and sound-event tags placed at semantically meaningful positions in Stage 2 script |
| Channels | Two background channels per dialogue, each paired with one speech channel, combining environmental noise and sound events |
| Environmental bank | Self-built bank of 7 scene categories and 600 long-duration clips; each noise instance persists for several turns (decided at script generation time) |
| Event bank | 52 categories and 2,203 short-duration clips; each event tag matched to an asset and inserted at the FA-aligned trigger position |
| Loudness | Both background noise and sound-event loudness controlled by configurable SNR |
| Pairing | Each speech channel pairs with a time-aligned background channel |
| Training note | "Training a full-duplex model only requires the background channel at the input (HM) side." |
| Licensing | "All audio assets are drawn from publicly accessible sources and processed strictly for non-commercial, academic research purposes." |
