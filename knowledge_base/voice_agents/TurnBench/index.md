# [2608.25218] TurnBench: A Multi-Domain Benchmark for Turn-Taking Dynamics in Spoken Dialogue

TurnBench is a multi-domain benchmark for conversational turn-taking, pairing a 30-hour triple-annotated dyadic corpus with a standardized end-of-turn and interruption detection protocol. Its headline result is a robustness split: end-of-turn recall is stable across six interaction styles while interruption false positives concentrate in backchannel-dense styles, and no tested system matches the human median of starting smooth floor transfers 151 ms early.
