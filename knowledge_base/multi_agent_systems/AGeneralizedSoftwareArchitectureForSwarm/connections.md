# Connections

- [[research_topics/multi_agent_systems/LLMTeamsAsDistributedSystems/summary|Language Model Teams as Distributed Systems]] — direct-theoretical-counterpart and contrast: applies distributed-systems theory (Amdahl's Law speedup bounds, centralized vs. decentralized coordination trade-offs, Raft-style consensus for fault tolerance) to LLM teams and finds centralized coordination dominates on consistency and token cost, while the swarm paper proposes exactly the decentralized design (eventual-consistency gossip+CRDT state, on-demand Raft quorum only for irreversible commitments) — read together to weigh the swarm's resilience/scalability claims against measured consistency-violation and cost penalties of self-coordinating teams.
- [[research_topics/multi_agent_systems/UsableAgentDiscovery/summary|Usable Agent Discovery for Decentralized AI Systems]] — shares-mechanism: empirically compares structured Kademlia DHT vs. gossip-based (Cyclon+Vicinity) overlays for agent discovery under two-level (node + agent-state) churn with a useful-availability metric, directly informing the swarm's membership-plane choice of a structured peer-to-peer overlay plus anti-entropy gossip and a SWIM-style failure detector — including the caution that neither overlay family dominates across all churn regimes.
- [[research_topics/multi_agent_systems/Ruflo/summary|Ruflo]] — applies-in-practice contrast: a production 15-agent hierarchical mesh swarm on Claude Code with capability-matched routing and vector memory, i.e. the centralized/hierarchical coordination philosophy the swarm paper diagnoses as a single point of failure and throughput bottleneck — same capability-routing problem, opposite answer to "who decides allocation."
- [[research_topics/multi_agent_systems/ClaudeSwarm/summary|claude-swarm]] — shares-pattern-different-coordination: also decomposes goals into a dependency DAG of subtasks executed in parallel waves, but under a central orchestrator with file-lock conflict handling, versus the swarm's decentralized capability-aware bidding (utility over capability fit, load, reputation) with deterministic-ID conflict resolution and CRDT merge — useful minimal pair on orchestrated vs. protocol-driven DAG execution.
- [[research_topics/graph_rag/ArxivGraphPlanner/summary|GraphPlanner: Graph Memory-Augmented Agentic Routing for Multi-Agent LLMs]] — shares-problem (capability/cost-aware routing across heterogeneous models) with a different method: a learned graph-memory routing policy trained over workflow traces, versus the swarm's hand-tuned scalar bidding utility `u = α·cap + β·(1−L) + γ·κ` with tempered greedy/softmax selection — a plausible upgrade path for the swarm's bidding engine and a check on the limits the swarm paper itself notes for its scalar utility.
