# Connections

- [[research/LeapSuperchargingLLMsFormalMathematics/summary|LEAP: Supercharging LLMs for Formal Mathematics with Agentic Frameworks]] — same-problem-different-method: also an agentic framework that decomposes proofs into a DAG of subproblems (AND-OR DAG with shared lemma nodes) with verification-guided search and reviewer filtering; both systems were even demonstrated on Knuth's Hamiltonian/cycles decomposition problem, making this the closest formal-math counterpart to compare decomposition and verification design against.
- [[research/VerificationHorizon/summary|The Verification Horizon: No Silver Bullet for Coding Agent Rewards]] — shares-technique: treats verification (not generation) as the binding constraint and prescribes verifier-generator co-evolution; directly relevant to Colosseum's global verifier with its two return paths (conservative revision vs. re-exploration) and its acknowledged open problem of credit assignment in long-horizon multi-agent settings.
- [[agent_harness/ScalingLawsAgentHarnesses/summary|Scaling Laws for Agent Harnesses Via Effective Feedback Compute]] — same-problem-different-emphasis: proposes Effective Feedback Compute (EFC), crediting harness feedback only when informative, valid, non-redundant, and retained; the natural quantitative lens for Colosseum's fixed population widths and its proposed future work on adaptive inference allocation with compute-matched evaluation.
- [[training_and_self_evolution/LongHorizonLLMTraining/summary|On Training Large Language Models for Long-Horizon Tasks]] — shares-problem: identifies task horizon length as an independent bottleneck and shows macro actions plus subgoal decomposition with dense rewards stabilize long-horizon behavior; the training-time mirror of Colosseum's inference-time answer (readiness-gated strategy exploration, dependency-DAG decomposition, local retry).
- [[research/CompilingAgenticWorkflowsIntoLLMWeights/summary|Compiling Agentic Workflows into LLM Weights]] — future-work counterpart: demonstrates distilling inference-time orchestration into model weights at 100x+ lower cost; the direct precedent for Colosseum's proposal to use validated research trajectories (strategies, critiques, revision histories) as post-training data, including the same credit-assignment challenge Colosseum flags.
