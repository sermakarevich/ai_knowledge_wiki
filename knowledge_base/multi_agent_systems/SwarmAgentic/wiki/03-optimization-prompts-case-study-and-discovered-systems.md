# Optimization Prompts, Case Study and Best-Discovered Systems
**In one sentence:** This chunk publishes the verbatim global-best, personal-best, velocity-update and position-update prompts that drive SwarmAgentic refinement, walks through a travel-planning case study where each guidance signal fixes a distinct defect, and presents the final best-discovered executable systems for four tasks alongside a detailed comparison showing why the Automated Design of Agentic Systems workflow fails on accommodation constraints.
## Key points
- Role refinement uses three operations, namely Add Role with name, responsibility and policy when a subtask overburdens existing roles, Modify Role for manageable policy refinements within scope, and Delete Role for redundant or conflicting roles.
- Workflow refinement uses five operations, namely Add Step with role plus upstream-output input plus expected output, Modify Input to incorporate prior outputs, Modify Output to align deliverables with downstream needs, Delete Step for redundant steps, and Re-order Steps to fix sequencing without breaking dependencies.
- Global-best learning with LLMglob and personal-best learning with LLMpers both force a four-part response per flaw of Identified Flaw, Thought, Comparative Insights with explicit quoted phrases, and Proposed Adjustment that reuses only quoted elements or says None.
- The illustrative travel-planning run shows global-best guidance adding a Quality Assurance Specialist for final review, personal-best guidance adding an Accommodation Coordinator cross-verification step between Step 2 and Step 3, and failure-driven adjustment hardening the Quality Assurance policy with a mandatory checklist plus explicit budget-compliance item 6.
- Best-discovered systems are published as executable forward code with 6 steps and 5 roles for Multilingual Grade School Math, 10 steps and 10 roles for Creative Writing, 7 steps and 7 roles for Meeting Scheduling, and 8 steps with 5 roles for TravelPlanner ending in a Travel Plan Integrator.
- The Automated Design of Agentic Systems TravelPlanner workflow uses Itinerary Planner, Budget Manager, Dining Advisor, Activity Coordinator and Meta Decision Agent with collaborative negotiation but no dedicated accommodation agent, so it cannot reliably enforce room type, minimum night stays, occupancy limits or pet-friendliness and often violates global requirements despite locally valid proposals.
- The chunk attributes Automated Design of Agentic Systems search difficulty to an ever-growing archive of full workflow definitions that exhausts context, accumulates irrelevant detail, and prioritizes novelty over optimization so truly optimal workflows are hard to identify.

## Shared role and workflow operations
The chunk opens on pages 26 through 29 by repeating the same operation catalog across the global-best, personal-best and velocity-update prompts so every optimizer speaks the same edit language. Role operations are Add Role for specialized responsibilities that cannot be folded into current roles without disrupting primary duties, Modify Role for policy-only refinements that stay within the role scope and do not overburden it, and Delete Role for redundant, unnecessary or conflicting roles. Workflow operations are Add Step only when a genuine gap hinders efficiency, coordination or goal achievement and the new step adds clear non-duplicative value with defined role, input taken from prior-step outputs and expected output, Modify Input so the step comprehensively incorporates prior outputs, Modify Output so deliverables align with the step purpose and downstream inputs, Delete Step when a step is redundant or overlapping without harming completeness, and Re-order Steps when sequencing causes inefficiency while preserving logical dependencies. This shared catalog matters because velocity plans, personal-best proposals and global-best proposals all compile down to the same executable edits applied later by the position-update operator.
## Learning from the global best with LLMglob

For each flaw the model must emit Identified Flaw as a clear statement, Thought asking what the global best team descriptions teach about that flaw, Comparative Insights extracting specific excellent elements as a structured sentence with explicit quotes from role responsibilities, role policies, step inputs, step outputs or step orders without commentary, assumptions or analysis, and Proposed Adjustment that directly reflects only the quoted phrases with no unrelated or rephrased ideas, saying None in both fields when nothing transfers. ## Learning from the personal best with LLMpers
The second prompt on pages 27 to 28 repeats the identical operation catalog and four-part response shape but swaps the reference from global best to personal best team placeholder p best, defined as the most effective setup for the same feedback seen along the trajectory from the initial team to the current team. The placeholders remain current team, task and feedback, and the response fields become Identified Flaw, Thought referencing personal best descriptions, Comparative Insights quoting role responsibilities, policies, inputs, outputs or orders from the personal best, and Proposed Adjustment reusing exactly those quoted phrases or None. The distinction is temporal and local versus global, since personal-best guidance mines the particle own history for fixes already proven on similar feedback while global-best guidance imports swarm-wide excellence, and together they balance self-learning against social learning in the velocity update.

## Illustrative optimization process
Appendix E.1 on pages 30 to 33 narrates one travel-planning trajectory where each velocity term contributes a distinct fix and lifts success rate from baseline toward 33 percent. Global-best guidance supplies the first major improvement by introducing a dedicated Quality Assurance Specialist role ensuring structural completeness and consistency across transportation, accommodation, dining and attractions before finalization. Personal-best guidance triggers the second improvement by detecting a missing verification between accommodation planning and downstream modules and inserting a cross-validation step where the Accommodation Coordinator verifies budget, minimum stays, child suitability, room availability and room count before forwarding data. Failure-driven adjustment then hardens the Quality Assurance module by adding budget-compliance checks after prior cost-violation failures, turning a vague review instruction into a mandatory checklist. The text stresses that task-dependency reordering proceeds in parallel and that the three signals jointly produce well-structured constraint-compliant workflows rather than any single fix dominating.

The personal-best example shows a previous team where Step 2 Accommodation Coordinator consumes a transportation plan and outputs nights, recommended hotels, minimum-stay confirmation, child suitability and room count, feeding Step 3 Restaurant Advisor directly with no validation. The LLMpers output names Identified Flaw as a missing review between Step 2 and Step 3, Thought as cross-verification preventing oversights, Comparative Insights quoting the personal best validation step to verify minimum stay requirements for all accommodations, and Proposed Adjustment adding a new Accommodation Coordinator cross-verification step after Step 2. ## Global-best and failure-driven examples
The global-best example starts from five roles without quality control, namely Transportation Planner, Accommodation Coordinator, Restaurant Advisor, Attraction Specialist and Travel Plan Integrator, and its LLMglob output names the flaw as lack of a final review, Thought as benefit of a comprehensive consistency review, Comparative Insights quoting the integrator policy to review the entire plan for consistency and completeness, and Proposed Adjustment introducing the Quality Assurance Specialist with responsibility to review the entire plan before finalization and a five-item policy covering transportation conflicts, accommodation minimum-stay and user constraints, restaurant preferences and dietary restrictions, attraction scheduling fit, and feedback for adjustments. The failure-driven example starts from that same specialist with the five-item policy and diagnoses Role Policy Deficiency as missing specific guidelines for verifying prior-step accuracy, causing budget discrepancies and incomplete itineraries. The documented Failed Adjustment had merely added a generic item 3 to review all outputs for consistency and completeness, while the improved Proposed Adjustment mandates a checklist-based verification process, compiled as retained items 1 through 5 plus new item 6 requiring verification of all components including accommodations, restaurants and attractions for user-constraint alignment and budgetary compliance.

## Comparison with the ADAS-discovered system
Appendix F.5 on pages 40 to 41 argues that Meta Agent Search archives every generated workflow so each new language-model prompt carries a long growing list of full definitions whose size, structural complexity and accumulated irrelevant detail exhaust context and muddle reasoning, while the novelty-seeking search enumerates limitless possibilities instead of optimizing toward the best workflow. Code 1 reproduces the Automated Design of Agentic Systems TravelPlanner program verbatim, defining itinerary, budget, dining, activity and dialogue instructions, instantiating Itinerary Planner, Budget Manager, Dining Advisor, Activity Coordinator and Meta Decision Agent via LLMAgentBase, gathering four parallel proposals from task info, running pairwise collaborative negotiation where each proposal is discussed against every other and updated from the second dialogue position, then asking the meta agent to evaluate proposals into a cohesive final plan. The critique is that no dedicated accommodation agent exists, so room type, minimum stays, occupancy and pet rules lack an explicit owner, joint multi-constraint reasoning fails, and the meta agent cannot coherently integrate locally valid proposals into a globally compliant plan, which explains the large empirical gap reported in the main results.
