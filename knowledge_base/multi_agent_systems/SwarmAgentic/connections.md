# Connections

- [[SelfRefine/summary|SelfRefine]] — Closest mechanism sibling: both rely on an LLM diagnosing flaws in plain language and rewriting behavior from that diagnosis with no weight updates, but Self-Refine loops generate→feedback→refine within a single output while SwarmAgentic lifts the loop to population search over whole teams with personal-best, global-best, and failure-memory guidance; SwarmAgentic's Creative Writing wins inherit Self-Refine's core hazard (an LLM judge scoring LLM-optimized outputs), and Self-Refine's Math Reasoning failure (the model rubber-stamps its own errors) is the reason to trust SwarmAgentic's exact-match MGSM and Natural Plan results more than its judge-scored Creative Writing column.
- [[Reflexion/summary|Reflexion]] — Shared verbal-feedback lineage with opposite memory design: Reflexion stores critiques across trials in persistent episodic memory keyed by task reward, while SwarmAgentic stores failure adjustments per particle plus personal/global bests across the swarm — SwarmAgentic's "quote the exact failed adjustment and forbid its reintroduction" is the stricter, string-level cousin of Reflexion's reflective memory, and both papers need external ground truth (a reward signal, an objective function) to know a trial failed at all.
- [[AgentSwarm/summary|Agent Swarm]] — Same "team that improves itself" promise at different layers: Agent Swarm is an engineering harness (lead agent dispatching Docker-isolated workers with compounding SQLite-backed memory, ~95% self-generated PRs) while SwarmAgentic is a discovery algorithm (swarm search over role/workflow designs from a task description); the compounding-memory layer of the former is what the latter lacks — SwarmAgentic rediscovers good structures every run from scratch, and pairing discovery with persistent cross-run memory would address its cost and small-training-sample fragility.
- [[AGeneralizedSoftwareArchitectureForSwarm/summary|A Generalized Software Architecture for Swarm]] — Architectural counterpoint: the generalized swarm architecture paper asks how to structure swarms as systems (coordination, interfaces, scaling), while SwarmAgentic asks how to discover what each swarm member should be — the two compose naturally, with SwarmAgentic generating the roles and workflows that a principled swarm runtime would then execute and govern.
- [[MechanicsOfSwarm/summary|Mechanics of Swarm]] — Theory anchor for the bio-inspired move: where Mechanics of Swarm studies how decentralized agents with local rules produce collective behavior, SwarmAgentic inverts the direction by searching over agent designs with centralized LLM guidance (personal/global bests) to produce a team — useful for asking whether the discovered Quality Assurance Specialist plus integrator pattern is a general attractor of swarm-designed teams or a TravelPlanner-specific artifact.
- [[Agentflow/summary|Agentflow]] — Workflow-optimization kin: Agentflow compiles or refines agentic workflows as first-class objects, which is the same level SwarmAgentic's position-update operator works at (Add/Modify/Delete/Reorder steps with repaired inputs, outputs, and order) — SwarmAgentic's contribution on top is the failure-aware velocity planning step and the mandatory-quotation grounding that keep workflow edits tied to diagnosed flaws rather than free invention.
- [[CompilingAgenticWorkflowsIntoLLMWeights/summary|Compiling Agentic Workflows into LLM Weights]] — Opposite end of the interpretability trade: Compiling Agentic Workflows distills workflow structure into model weights (opaque but cheap at inference), while SwarmAgentic emits modular interpretable teams (inspectable but expensive to discover and run) — the pair frames the real deployment question the paper never answers, namely when a discovered 8-step 5-role team is worth its inference cost versus compiling it away.
- [[MetaAgentChallenge/summary|Meta Agent Challenge]] — Evaluation-lens sibling: the Meta Agent Challenge work interrogates how to benchmark meta/agentic-search methods fairly (seeds, budgets, judge independence), which is exactly the lens SwarmAgentic's 261.8%-over-ADAS headline needs — matched inference budgets, fixed training subsamples, and non-LLM-judge scoring are the controls that would turn this paper's strong result into a durable one.
