# Order Changes

1. You may modify your order details, including shipping address and contact information, up to 24 hours after placing the order. After this 24-hour window, the order is locked in our system and cannot be edited manually. If you need to change the address after this period, you must cancel the order and place a new one, subject to our cancellation policy.

2. Changing the size or color of an item is permitted only if the new variant is in stock. If the requested variant is out of stock, we will offer a 10% discount on the next purchase as a goodwill gesture. This discount is applied automatically to your account and expires after 30 days if not used.

3. Upgrading your shipping method from Standard to Express incurs an additional fee of $15.00. This fee is calculated based on the difference in carrier rates. If you downgrade from Express to Standard, we will refund the difference, which is typically between $5.00 and $12.00, within 5 business days of the change request.

4. Adding items to an existing order is allowed only if the original order has not yet been shipped. You can add up to 3 additional items per order. Each added item must be under $50.00 in value. If the total order value exceeds $200.00 after additions, the standard free shipping threshold is met, and any previously charged shipping fees will be refunded.

5. Removing items from an order is free of charge if done before the order is processed for shipment. Once the order status changes to "Processing," removal is no longer possible. You must cancel the entire order and re-order the remaining items. Cancellation fees of $5.00 apply if the order has already been picked from the warehouse.

6. Payment method changes are restricted to credit cards and PayPal. You cannot switch to store credit or gift cards after the order is placed. If you change your payment method, the new card must be verified within 48 hours. Failure to verify results in the order being canceled and any pre-authorized funds being released back to your bank within 7 business days.

## Key facts

- You have a strict 24-hour window to edit your shipping address or contact details before the order locks.
- Upgrading to Express shipping costs an extra $15.00, while downgrading refunds the difference, usually between $5.00 and $12.00.
- You can add up to 3 items to an unshipped order, provided each item costs less than $50.00.
- Cancellation fees of $5.00 apply if you cancel after the order status becomes "Processing."
- Payment verification for new cards must be completed within 48 hours to avoid automatic cancellation.
