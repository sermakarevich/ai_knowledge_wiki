# Shipping

1. **Standard Delivery Timeframe**
   All standard shipping orders are processed within 1 business day of confirmation. Once dispatched, delivery to major metropolitan areas takes 3 to 5 business days. For rural or remote locations, please allow an additional 2 to 4 business days. We guarantee that 95% of all standard orders arrive within 7 business days of the processing date. If your order has not arrived within 10 business days, contact our support team immediately for a tracking investigation.

2. **Expedited Shipping Options**
   Customers may select expedited shipping at checkout for faster delivery. Two-Day Air shipping costs a flat fee of $25.00 per order and guarantees delivery within 2 business days for most continental addresses. Overnight Express shipping is available for a fee of $45.00 and ensures delivery by 10:00 AM the next business day. Please note that expedited services are not available for orders containing items over 50 lbs, such as full-size mountain bikes, which must ship via standard freight.

3. **Free Shipping Threshold**
   To encourage larger purchases, Northwind Outdoor offers free standard shipping on all orders totaling $150.00 or more before tax and discounts. Orders below this threshold incur a standard shipping fee of $12.00. This free shipping benefit applies to all domestic addresses within the United States. International customers are not eligible for free shipping and will be charged a flat rate of $60.00 for standard international delivery, regardless of order value.

4. **Freight and Large Item Delivery**
   Bikes, tents, and other bulky camping gear are shipped via freight carriers. The base freight cost is $85.00 for a single large item. Additional large items incur a surcharge of $40.00 each. Freight delivery takes 5 to 8 business days. You must be present to sign for the delivery. If you are not available, the carrier will attempt delivery three times over 3 consecutive business days before returning the item to the warehouse, which may result in a restocking fee of 15% of the item’s value.

5. **Address Verification and Changes**
   You may change your shipping address within 24 hours of placing your order at no additional cost. After 24 hours, a $10.00 administrative fee applies to address changes. Once the tracking number has been generated and the package has left our warehouse, address changes are no longer possible. Please ensure your address is accurate at checkout to avoid delivery delays or failed delivery attempts.

## Key facts

- Free standard shipping applies to orders over $150.00.
- Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days in cities.
- Expedited 2-Day Air shipping costs $25.00.
- Freight shipping for bikes starts at $85.00.
- Address changes are free within 24 hours of purchase.
