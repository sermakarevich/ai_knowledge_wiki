# Damaged Items

1. You must report any visible damage to your Northwind Outdoor order within 48 hours of delivery. To initiate a claim, log into your account and select the "Report Damage" option. You are required to upload at least 3 high-resolution photos showing the specific defect, the overall item, and the shipping packaging. Failure to provide this visual evidence within the 48-hour window will result in an automatic denial of the claim, as we cannot verify the condition of the item after it has been used or stored.

2. If the damage is minor, such as a small scratch on a bike frame or a loose stitching on a tent fly, we will offer a 15% discount on your next purchase as a goodwill gesture. This discount is applied automatically to your account and is valid for 30 days. For major structural failures, such as a cracked wheel rim or a torn tent pole, we will issue a full refund of the item’s price, up to a maximum of $500.00 per item. Items exceeding this value require a manual review by our senior support team, which may take up to 5 business days to process.

3. When we approve a full refund or replacement, you must ship the damaged item back to our warehouse within 10 days of receiving the return authorization label. We will provide a prepaid shipping label for items under 20 lbs. For heavier items, such as mountain bikes or large camping stoves, you must arrange your own freight pickup. We will reimburse up to 50% of the shipping cost, capped at $75.00, provided you submit the original receipt within 14 days of the pickup date.

4. If the damage is caused by improper use, such as overloading a bike rack beyond its 150 lb capacity limit or using a camping stove in a non-ventilated area, the claim will be denied. We reserve the right to inspect the item upon return. If our inspection reveals signs of misuse or neglect, we will notify you within 3 business days. In such cases, you will be responsible for the return shipping costs, which typically range from $15.00 to $40.00 depending on the item’s weight and dimensions.

5. For items that are partially damaged but still functional, we may offer a repair service at no cost to you. This includes replacing broken zippers on backpacks or fixing bent handlebars on bikes. The repair process takes approximately 7 to 10 business days. During this time, you may request a loaner item if available, subject to a $20.00 deposit that is refunded upon the return of the loaner in good condition.

## Key facts

- You have 48 hours to report damage with 3 photos.
- Minor damage earns a 15% discount valid for 30 days.
- Full refunds are capped at $500.00 per item.
- You must return damaged items within 10 days.
- Misuse claims are denied, and you pay return shipping.
