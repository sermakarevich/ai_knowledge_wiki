# International Orders

1. **Shipping Costs and Thresholds**: Northwind Outdoor charges a flat shipping fee of $45.00 for all international orders under $150.00. For orders totaling $150.00 or more, standard international shipping is free. This threshold applies to the subtotal before any discounts are applied. Customers must select their country at checkout to see the exact delivery estimate, which typically ranges from 7 to 14 business days depending on the destination.

2. **Customs Duties and Taxes**: All international shipments are subject to local customs duties, import taxes, and handling fees. These charges are calculated by the carrier and are not included in the Northwind Outdoor checkout total. On average, customers in the European Union pay an additional 20% in VAT, while customers in other regions may face varying rates. Northwind Outdoor is not responsible for these third-party charges, and we recommend checking your local customs regulations before placing an order to avoid unexpected costs upon delivery.

3. **Return Window and Process**: You have 30 days from the date of delivery to initiate a return for international orders. This window is strictly enforced to account for transit times. To start a return, you must contact our support team within this 30-day period to receive a prepaid return label. If you miss this 30-day deadline, we cannot accept the return, and no refund will be issued. Please ensure your item is in its original condition with all tags attached before shipping it back.

4. **Refund Processing Time**: Once we receive your returned item at our warehouse, it takes 5 to 7 business days to inspect and process the refund. After processing, it may take an additional 10 to 14 business days for the funds to appear in your bank account, depending on your financial institution. Please note that the original shipping fee of $45.00 is non-refundable unless the item was defective or we shipped the wrong product.

5. **Prohibited Items and Restrictions**: We do not ship lithium batteries, fuel canisters, or certain chemical cleaning agents to countries with strict aviation safety regulations. This includes most destinations in the Middle East and parts of Southeast Asia. If your order contains prohibited items, we will cancel the order and issue a full refund within 3 business days. We reserve the right to refuse service to any location where local laws prohibit the import of outdoor gear.

## Key facts

- Free international shipping applies to orders over $150.00.
- You have 30 days from delivery to request a return.
- Customs duties are extra and vary by country, often around 20%.
- Refunds take 5 to 7 business days to process after receipt.
- Original shipping fees are non-refundable for standard returns.
