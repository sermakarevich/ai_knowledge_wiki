# Accounts

1. **Account Creation and Verification**
   To create a Northwind Outdoor account, you must provide a valid email address and a password containing at least 8 characters, including one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, and one special character. We require email verification within 24 hours of registration. If your email is not verified within this 24-hour window, your account will be automatically suspended. You may reactivate it by resending the verification link, but if it remains unverified for 7 days, the account is permanently deleted and any associated draft orders are canceled.

2. **Password Security and Recovery**
   For your security, we enforce a maximum of 5 failed login attempts per hour. If you exceed this threshold, your account is locked for 30 minutes. To reset your password, you must use the "Forgot Password" link, which sends a unique reset code valid for 15 minutes. You must change your password every 180 days if you have enabled two-factor authentication (2FA). Failure to update your password after the 180-day mark will result in a mandatory password reset prompt upon your next login attempt.

3. **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**
   We strongly recommend enabling 2FA for all accounts with a lifetime purchase history exceeding $500. When 2FA is active, you must enter a 6-digit code from your authenticator app or SMS to log in. If you lose access to your 2FA device, you must contact support and provide a government-issued ID. Support will verify your identity within 48 hours. During this verification period, your account access is restricted to read-only mode, meaning you can view orders but cannot place new ones or change billing details.

4. **Account Deletion and Data Retention**
   You may request account deletion at any time via the "Account Settings" page. Upon request, we will send a confirmation email. If you do not confirm within 10 days, the request is voided. If you do confirm, your personal data is retained for 30 days to allow for a final "cooling-off" period. After this 30-day window, all personal data, including order history and contact information, is permanently purged from our servers. However, financial transaction records are retained for 7 years to comply with tax regulations, regardless of your deletion request.

5. **Multiple Accounts and Fraud Prevention**
   Each customer is limited to one active account. Creating multiple accounts to circumvent return limits, abuse promotional codes, or manipulate loyalty points is strictly prohibited. If our fraud detection system identifies more than 2 accounts linked to the same IP address or payment method within a 24-hour period, all associated accounts are flagged for review. This review process takes up to 5 business days. During this time, any pending orders are held, and refunds are paused until the review is complete.

## Key facts

- Email verification must be completed within 24 hours of registration to avoid suspension.
- Accounts are locked for 30 minutes after 5 failed login attempts.
- Passwords must be changed every 180 days if two-factor authentication is enabled.
- Account deletion requests have a 30-day cooling-off period before data is purged.
- Financial records are retained for 7 years even after account deletion.
