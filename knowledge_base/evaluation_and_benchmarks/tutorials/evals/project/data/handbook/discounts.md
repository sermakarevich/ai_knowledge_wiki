# Discounts

1. **Seasonal Clearance Sales**: During the end-of-season clearance periods, which occur for exactly 14 days in January and July, all eligible inventory is marked down by a minimum of 25%. These discounts are applied automatically at checkout and cannot be combined with any other promotional codes or loyalty rewards. Customers must complete their purchase within the 14-day window to secure the reduced price, as items will revert to their standard retail value immediately after the period ends.

2. **Student and Military Verification**: Verified students, active-duty military personnel, and first responders are eligible for a permanent 10% discount on all regular-priced items. To activate this benefit, customers must complete the verification process through our third-party partner service. Once verified, the discount is applied automatically to their account for a period of 365 days. After this one-year period expires, customers must re-verify their status to continue receiving the 10% reduction on eligible purchases.

3. **Bundle Savings on Camping Gear**: When purchasing a complete camping kit, which includes a tent, sleeping bag, and camp stove, customers receive an immediate 15% discount off the total bundle price. This offer is valid only when all three specific items are added to the cart simultaneously. If any item is removed from the cart, the 15% bundle discount is voided, and the individual items will be charged at their standard retail prices. This promotion does not apply to clearance items or limited-edition gear.

4. **Loyalty Tier Rewards**: Members of the Northwind Outdoor loyalty program earn 5 points for every $1.00 spent. Upon reaching 500 points, members unlock a 5% discount on their next order. Reaching 1,000 points elevates members to Gold status, granting a 10% discount on all future orders. Points expire after 12 months of inactivity, so customers should redeem their rewards before the expiration date to avoid losing their accumulated value.

5. **Referral Program Incentives**: Existing customers can earn a $20.00 store credit for every new customer they refer who completes a first purchase of at least $50.00. The referred customer also receives a $10.00 discount on their initial order. Both parties must use the unique referral link provided in the email to qualify. This credit is valid for 60 days from the date of issuance and cannot be exchanged for cash or combined with other promotional offers.

## Key facts

- Clearance sales last exactly 14 days and offer a minimum 25% discount.
- Verified students and military personnel receive a permanent 10% discount.
- Buying a tent, sleeping bag, and stove together saves 15% on the total.
- Loyalty members earn 5 points per $1.00 spent, with points expiring in 12 months.
- Referrals earn $20.00 credit for new customers spending at least $50.00.
