# Loyalty Program

1. **Membership Enrollment and Cost**
   Joining the Northwind Outdoor Loyalty Program is completely free for all customers. There is no annual fee, no hidden subscription cost, and no minimum purchase requirement to maintain your active status. You can sign up instantly during checkout or via your account dashboard. Once enrolled, your membership remains active indefinitely as long as you have made at least one qualifying purchase within the last 365 days. If you do not make a purchase within this 1-year window, your account will be automatically downgraded to a guest status, and all accumulated points will be frozen for 90 days before potential expiration.

2. **Earning Points on Purchases**
   You earn 1 loyalty point for every $1.00 spent on eligible items. This includes all bikes, camping gear, accessories, and apparel sold directly by Northwind Outdoor. Points are credited to your account within 24 hours of the order being shipped. Please note that points are not earned on gift cards, store credit, or items purchased during clearance sales marked with a "No Points" tag. Additionally, any order that is subsequently returned or cancelled will have the corresponding points deducted from your balance. If your balance drops below zero due to returns, you must make a new purchase to restore your standing before redeeming rewards.

3. **Point Expiration Policy**
   Loyalty points are valid for 12 months from the date they were earned. For example, if you earn 500 points on January 15, 2024, those specific points will expire on January 14, 2025. We do not extend expiration dates for points that have already been earned. To keep your points active, you simply need to make a new purchase that generates new points. We recommend checking your account dashboard monthly to see your current balance and the expiration dates of your oldest points. This ensures you maximize the value of your rewards before they lapse.

4. **Redeeming Rewards**
   You can redeem your accumulated points for discount codes at checkout. The conversion rate is fixed: 100 points equal a $5.00 discount. Therefore, you need a minimum of 100 points to redeem your first reward. There is no maximum limit on how many points you can redeem in a single transaction, provided you have sufficient balance. Discount codes generated from points cannot be combined with other promotional codes, such as seasonal sales or newsletter sign-up bonuses. Once a code is applied to an order, it is locked to that transaction and cannot be transferred or refunded if the order is cancelled.

5. **Tier Advancement and Benefits**
   Our program features two tiers: Standard and Elite. You start as a Standard member. To reach Elite status, you must accumulate 5,000 points in a rolling 12-month period. Elite members enjoy a 10% bonus on all points earned, meaning you effectively earn 1.1 points per $1.00 spent. Elite status is reviewed quarterly. If you fail to maintain the 5,000-point threshold in the subsequent 12-month window, you will revert to Standard status. Elite members also receive early access to new product launches, typically 48 hours before the general public.

## Key facts

- Membership is free with no annual fee.
- You earn 1 point for every $1.00 spent.
- Points expire 12 months after being earned.
- 100 points equal a $5.00 discount.
- Elite status requires 5,000 points in 12 months.
