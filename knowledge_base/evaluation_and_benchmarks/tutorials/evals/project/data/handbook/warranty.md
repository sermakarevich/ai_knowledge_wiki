# Warranty

Northwind Outdoor stands behind the quality of every bike and camping item we sell. Our warranty policy is designed to give you peace of mind while you explore the outdoors. Please review the following terms carefully before making a purchase.

1. **Standard Coverage Period**: All new bikes, tents, and sleeping bags come with a standard 2-year warranty from the date of original purchase. This coverage includes defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. If a defect is found within this 730-day window, we will repair or replace the item at no cost to you.

2. **Extended Warranty Option**: For an additional 15% of the original purchase price, you can upgrade to a 5-year extended warranty. This option is available only within the first 30 days of purchase. The extended warranty covers the same defects as the standard plan but extends the protection period to 1,825 days, ensuring long-term reliability for high-value gear.

3. **Repair Service Fees**: If your item is outside the warranty period or the damage is due to misuse, we offer a repair service. A flat diagnostic fee of $25 applies to all repair requests. If you approve the repair, this $25 fee is credited toward the final invoice. If you decline the repair, the diagnostic fee is non-refundable.

4. **Wear and Tear Exclusions**: Normal wear and tear, such as fabric fading, minor scratches, or zipper stiffness, is not covered under warranty. However, if a zipper fails completely within the first 90 days, we will replace the zipper free of charge. After 90 days, zipper replacements cost $15 per unit, plus shipping.

5. **Claim Processing Time**: Once we receive your warranty claim and the item, we will inspect it within 5 business days. If the claim is approved, we will ship the replacement or repaired item within 10 business days of the inspection completion. Please allow an additional 3 to 5 days for transit depending on your location.

6. **Transferability**: The standard 2-year warranty is non-transferable. If you sell your Northwind Outdoor gear, the warranty voids immediately upon transfer of ownership. However, the extended 5-year warranty is transferable to one subsequent owner, provided the new owner registers the item within 14 days of purchase.

## Key facts

- The standard warranty lasts for 2 years (730 days) from the purchase date.
- You can extend coverage to 5 years for 15% of the original price within 30 days.
- A $25 diagnostic fee applies to out-of-warranty repairs, credited if you proceed.
- Zipper failures within the first 90 days are replaced free of charge.
- Warranty claims are inspected within 5 business days of receipt.
