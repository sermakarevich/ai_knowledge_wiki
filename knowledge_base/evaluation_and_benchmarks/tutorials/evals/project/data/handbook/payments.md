# Payments

1. **Accepted Methods and Limits**: Northwind Outdoor accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay. For credit card transactions, the maximum single purchase amount is $5,000.00. If your order total exceeds this threshold, you must split the payment into multiple transactions or use an alternative payment method. We do not accept cash, checks, or cryptocurrency. All transactions are processed securely through our PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway to ensure your financial data remains protected at all times.

2. **Authorization and Capture Timing**: When you place an order, we immediately authorize the full amount on your payment method. This authorization hold typically lasts for 3 to 5 business days. The actual capture of funds occurs only after we have shipped your items. If your order is canceled before shipment, the authorization will be released automatically within 7 business days, depending on your bank’s processing schedule. Please note that some banks may take up to 10 business days to remove the hold from your available balance.

3. **Currency and Exchange Rates**: All prices on the Northwind Outdoor website are listed in US Dollars (USD). If your bank or card issuer charges a foreign transaction fee, this cost is not included in the displayed price and is determined by your financial institution. We do not apply any additional markup for currency conversion. For international customers, please verify with your bank that they support cross-border transactions to avoid declined payments. We recommend using a card that offers zero foreign transaction fees to save up to 3% on your total purchase.

4. **Failed Payment Handling**: If a payment is declined due to insufficient funds or a security flag, the order is automatically canceled. You have 48 hours to retry the payment with a different method. If you do not complete the payment within this window, the reserved inventory is released back to the general stock pool. We do not hold items for failed transactions beyond this 48-hour period. Multiple failed attempts within 24 hours may result in a temporary suspension of your account for 7 days for security review.

5. **Refund Processing Times**: Refunds are issued to the original payment method only. For credit and debit cards, refunds appear on your statement within 5 to 10 business days after we process the return. PayPal refunds are typically visible within 24 hours. If you paid with a gift card, the refund is issued as store credit valid for 12 months. We do not issue refunds to different payment methods or as cash. Please allow an additional 3 business days for your bank to post the credit after we have initiated the refund.

## Key facts

- Maximum credit card transaction limit is $5,000.00.
- Authorization holds last 3 to 5 business days.
- Failed payments must be retried within 48 hours.
- Card refunds take 5 to 10 business days to appear.
- Foreign transaction fees are charged by your bank, not us.
