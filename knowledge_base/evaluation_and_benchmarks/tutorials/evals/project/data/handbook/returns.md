# Returns

1. You have a strict 30-day window from the date of delivery to initiate a return request. If you miss this deadline, we cannot accept the item back, and no refund will be issued. This period is non-negotiable to ensure fair inventory management for our warehouse staff.
2. All returned items must be in their original, unused condition with all tags, manuals, and protective packaging intact. If the item shows signs of wear, dirt, or damage, we will deduct a 15% restocking fee from your refund amount. This fee covers the labor required to inspect and repackage the goods for resale.
3. Standard ground shipping costs for returns are the customer’s responsibility. We offer a prepaid return label for a flat fee of $12.50. If you choose to ship via an expedited carrier, you must pay the full shipping cost upfront, and we will only reimburse the standard rate upon receipt of the item.
4. Refunds are processed within 5 business days after we receive and inspect your package. The funds will appear in your original payment method within an additional 3 to 7 business days, depending on your bank’s processing times. Please note that credit card issuers may take up to 10 days to post the credit.
5. High-value items, specifically those priced over $500, require a signature upon delivery. If you are not present to sign for the package, the carrier will leave a notice. You have 48 hours to retrieve the item from the local depot. If you fail to collect it within this timeframe, the item is considered returned, and the return process begins immediately.
6. We do not accept returns on custom-configured bikes or personalized gear. These items are made to order and cannot be resold. If you ordered the wrong size or color, you are responsible for the full cost of the item, which is non-refundable. Please double-check your specifications before confirming your order.

## Key facts

- The return window is exactly 30 days from the delivery date.
- A 15% restocking fee applies if the item is not in perfect, unused condition.
- The prepaid return label costs a flat fee of $12.50.
- Refunds are processed within 5 business days of receiving the package.
- Items priced over $500 require a signature and must be collected within 48 hours if missed.
