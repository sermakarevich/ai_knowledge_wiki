# Gift Cards

1. **Purchase Limits and Minimums**
   You may purchase a single digital or physical gift card with a minimum value of $25.00 and a maximum value of $500.00 per transaction. If you wish to send a gift card for an amount exceeding $500.00, you must contact our customer support team directly to arrange a custom order. There is no limit to the number of gift cards you can buy in a single day, provided each individual card meets the minimum value requirement. This structure allows you to split larger gifts into multiple smaller cards if you prefer, ensuring flexibility for different recipients.

2. **Delivery Timeframes**
   Digital gift cards are delivered via email within 15 minutes of completing your purchase. Physical gift cards are shipped within 3 business days of order confirmation. Please note that shipping times do not include weekends or public holidays. If you select expedited shipping, the delivery window is reduced to 1 business day. We recommend ordering physical cards at least 5 days before your intended gift date to account for potential carrier delays. Tracking information is provided via email as soon as the item leaves our warehouse.

3. **Expiration and Fees**
   Northwind Outdoor gift cards do not expire. There are no monthly maintenance fees, inactivity fees, or dormancy charges associated with holding a gift card balance. Your funds remain available for use indefinitely. This policy ensures that you can purchase a gift card well in advance of a holiday or special occasion without worrying about the value diminishing over time. We believe in long-term value for our customers, so your balance is preserved exactly as you paid it, minus any applicable sales tax collected at the time of purchase.

4. **Refund Policy**
   Unused gift cards can be refunded to the original payment method within 30 days of the purchase date. Once a gift card has been partially used, the remaining balance cannot be refunded to cash or credit. Instead, the unused portion remains on the card for future use. If a gift card was purchased with a credit card, the refund will be processed to that same credit card. Refunds for gift cards purchased with other payment methods, such as PayPal or debit cards, will be issued as store credit. Please allow 5 to 7 business days for the refund to appear in your account.

5. **Lost or Stolen Cards**
   If you believe your physical gift card has been lost or stolen, contact customer support immediately. We can freeze the card and issue a replacement if you can provide the original purchase receipt and the last four digits of the card number. There is a $5.00 administrative fee for replacing a lost or stolen physical card. Digital gift cards are tied to your email address and can be re-sent to your inbox upon request. We cannot replace a digital card if it has already been redeemed, as the transaction is considered complete.

## Key facts

- Minimum purchase amount is $25.00 per card.
- Digital cards arrive within 15 minutes of purchase.
- Gift cards never expire and have no monthly fees.
- Unused cards are refundable within 30 days of purchase.
- Replacing a lost physical card costs $5.00.
