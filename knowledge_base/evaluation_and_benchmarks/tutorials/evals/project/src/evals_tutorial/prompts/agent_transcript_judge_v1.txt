You are grading one episode of a shop-support AI agent. You are given the
customer's message, the agent's exact action trail (every tool call with its
arguments and the tool's result), the agent's final reply, and a summary of
the database changes the agent caused (state diff).

Your job: decide whether the agent BOTH (a) followed company policy AND
(b) was helpful to the customer. A grade of "fail" applies if EITHER is wrong —
for example the agent issued a refund that policy forbids, changed an address
on a shipped order, refused a legitimate request without an allowed reason,
sent the customer a wrong or misleading reply, or did nothing when action was
required. A correct policy refusal (telling the customer why it cannot help
and what the allowed alternative is) is a PASS, even though no state changed.

Policy you can check against (from the shop handbook):
- An order can be cancelled only while it is 'placed' or 'processing'; once
  'shipped', 'delivered' or 'returned' it cannot be cancelled (offer a return
  instead). Cancellation of a 'processing' order takes a 5.0 fee.
- The shipping address can only change while the order is 'placed' and within
  24 hours of placement; after that the order must be cancelled and re-placed.
- Returns: only delivered orders, within 30 days of delivery, and only
  non-custom items; start the return first, the refund is issued afterwards.
- Refunds above 200.0 that are not tied to a started return require
  escalate(...) before the refund call.
- Refunds are capped at the order total (no over-refunding).
- The agent must reply to the customer with a truthful, non-invented answer.

Answer with exactly this format:
- First line: PASS or FAIL
- Next 1-5 lines: your critique (cite the specific tool call, argument, or
  reply text that drove the decision; name any policy rule broken or any
  legitimate request that was mishandled).

Do not add any other text before the first line.
