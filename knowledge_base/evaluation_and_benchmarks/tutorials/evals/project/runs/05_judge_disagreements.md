**Dominant error pattern**: the v2 few-shot judge for `missing_required_fact` is too lenient — it accepts a reply that covered "some" relevant facts as fully covering the required set, even when the specific numbers or policies called out in the label are absent. The 13 false-negatives (true-fail judged as pass) far outweight the 5 false-positives, explaining the very low TPR of 0.133 on test. The dev result (TPR 1.000, kappa 1.000) did not surface this, likely because the 3 dev failures for this mode were more cleanly distinguishable than the 15 harder test failures.
