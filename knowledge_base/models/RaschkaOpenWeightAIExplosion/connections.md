# Connections

- [[models/Nemotron3Super/summary|Nemotron 3 Super]] — Raschka explicitly names Nemotron 3 Ultra's LatentMoE as the closest architectural analogue to Kimi K3's latent MoE layer ([[wiki/01-kimi-k3-architecture|Kimi K3's Architecture]]); Nemotron3Super's own summary documents the same compressed-routing idea (LatentMoE) plus Mamba-2 hybrid attention layers, which this video's [[wiki/02-architecture-innovations-hybrid-attention|hybrid-attention page]] also cites as one of the three named examples of the linear-attention trend (alongside Qwen3-Next's Gated DeltaNet and Kimi Delta Attention).
- [[agent_harness/HarnessUpdatingNotHarnessBenefit/summary|Harness Updating Is Not Harness Benefit]] — a formal study of exactly the model-harness coupling question Raschka and Hugo speculate about informally in [[wiki/04-harness-model-coupling-and-overfitting|Harness-Model Coupling and the Overfitting Question]]; where the podcast treats "does the model overfit to its native harness" as an open, untested hunch, this paper actually disentangles harness-updating skill from harness-benefit skill across 7 LLMs and finds a non-monotonic (U-shaped) benefit curve by capability tier — a useful empirical check on the podcast's speculation.
- [[agent_harness/SmallLanguageModelsAreTheFutureOfAgenticAi/summary|Small Language Models are the Future of Agentic AI]] — argues from an economics/capability angle for exactly the shift toward local/small models that [[wiki/05-local-and-self-hosted-inference|Local and Self-Hosted Inference]] treats mainly as a fun/privacy/hedge choice today; this paper's claim that modern SLMs already match 30-70B models on narrow agentic sub-tasks is a more optimistic, structured counterpart to Raschka's more hobbyist framing of local inference.
- [[KimiCodeDocs/summary|Kimi Code Documentation]] — documents Moonshot AI's own coding-agent CLI/harness, the concrete "native harness" Raschka says he'd reach for when using Kimi models ([[wiki/04-harness-model-coupling-and-overfitting|Harness-Model Coupling and the Overfitting Question]]); reading the two together shows both sides of the same claim — the podcast's assertion that native harnesses perform best, and the actual harness being described.
