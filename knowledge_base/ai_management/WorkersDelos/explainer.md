# Workers Delos — Autonomous AI Workers, 24/7 — In Plain Language

## What is this about?

Workers Delos (delos.so), built by the European company Delos, lets you
"hire" AI workers — digital teammates that do multi-step jobs on their
own, day and night, instead of just answering chat questions.

Each worker is an autonomous AI agent (a software helper that carries
out tasks by itself once you give it a goal). Every worker has three
things that make it feel like a colleague:

1. A personality — its own tone and working style, tuned to its role
   (for example James in marketing talks ROI — Return on Investment —
   first; Laura the developer cites PR — Pull Request — numbers).
2. Specialized skills — training for one kind of job, such as
   marketing, software development, design, data, HR (Human Resources —
   the team that hires and supports people), finance, or support.
3. A memory — it remembers your preferences, templates, and past chats,
   so you do not repeat yourself every time.

The catalog holds 15 ready-made profiles (James, Laura, Sophie, Atif,
Nova, Karen, Sun, Ivan, Victoria, Henry, Mateo, Rosa, Jeanne, Alexa,
Alex, Camille, Arjun and others rotate through the catalog pages).
The company says more than 10,000 workers are already deployed.
You can hire one worker, assemble a team of 1 to 15 that shares context
and coordinates, or train a personal clone — a digital twin that writes
and reasons in your own style from your emails, messages, and docs.

## Why does it matter?

Most teams are overloaded: campaigns, tickets, designs, reports, hiring,
and support pile up, while hiring people is slow and expensive.

Ordinary chatbots do not fix this. They answer questions but do not sit
inside your workflow and finish jobs end to end while you do other work.

Workers Delos tries to close that gap with a simple promise: hand a
mission to a worker inside the tools you already use — Slack or
Microsoft Teams (team chat apps), Gmail, Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce,
Jira, GitHub, Figma — and it executes, keeps you posted in-channel, and
learns from your corrections. Every automation stays traceable, and a
human still validates important decisions.

Trying it is low-risk: there is a free starting option, no minimum
commitment, workers can be added or removed anytime, and a blog with
field reports, templates, and checklists shows how other teams use them.

## How does it work?

Hiring a worker takes less than five minutes and needs no code:

1. Pick a profile from the catalog (or combine several into a team, or
   start a personal clone from your writing and reference documents).
2. Connect your tools. Workers plug into Slack, Teams, Gmail, Outlook,
   Notion, Google Drive, HubSpot, Salesforce, Jira, GitHub, Figma, and
   3000+ others via OAuth (an industry login standard that grants access
   without sharing your password). You set what each worker may read or
   write, tool by tool, and can revoke it anytime.
3. Describe the missions to hand off in plain words — the worker starts
   executing right away, reports in your channels, and escalates to a
   human when needed.

Under the hood the stack is standard modern practice, well assembled:
latest-generation language models (GPT, Claude, Gemini families, auto-
switched per task), RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation — the AI first
looks up your documents, then answers from them, so replies are grounded
instead of invented), persistent memory isolated per customer, and
multi-agent workflows (several workers chaining tasks, e.g. marketing
briefs, design draws, SEO — Search Engine Optimization — polishes)
coordinated through open standards like MCP (Model Context Protocol — a
standard for connecting AI to tools). Tool calls are logged for audit,
and sensitive actions can require human approval.

There is also Companion, a browser extension for Chrome and Edge: writing
help, chat with your favourite language models about the page you are
on, and instant translation, right inside your inbox and every website.

On safety: data is encrypted end to end (scrambled so outsiders cannot
read it), kept in isolated environments (separate locked-off spaces per
customer), never used to train models. The service states GDPR (the
European Union privacy law) compliance and SOC 2 certification (an
independent security audit).

Pricing is flexible: a free start, an Office Suite at €10–€80 per month
(Student €10, Explore €25, Advanced €45, Premium €80), per-worker hiring,
and a custom Enterprise plan. The catalog's 200+ documented use cases —
each with recommended worker, tasks, and expected time savings — tell
you where to start: one high-impact, low-effort win first, then expand.

## Conclusions & takeaways

- Workers Delos turns AI from a chat helper into hired teammates — plus
  teams of up to 15 with shared memory, and personal clones — working
  end to end, 24/7, inside tools you already use.
- The experience edge is onboarding speed (under five minutes, no code),
  role-calibrated personalities, and in-channel operation with a
  Companion extension and 200+ guided use cases.
- The engineering underneath (language models + RAG + per-customer
  memory + logged, permissioned, human-approvable actions) is solid
  standard practice, not magic — its value is the packaging.
- Scale signals exist (15 profiles, 200+ use cases, 10,000+ workers
  claimed) but proof is thin: treat vendor numbers as marketing until
  verified by a trial.
- Start small and reversible: one worker, one workflow, read-only first,
  approvals on writes, check early work closely, expand only on measured
  time saved and low error rates.

|---|---|
| AI Worker | A digital teammate: software that does multi-step jobs alone inside your tools. |
| Autonomous agent | A helper that runs tasks by itself once given a goal, without step-by-step orders. |
| RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) | The AI first looks up your documents, then writes an answer based on them. |
| Persistent memory | The worker remembers your preferences and history between sessions. |
| Multi-agent workflow | Several workers chaining tasks, e.g. marketing briefs, design draws, SEO polishes. |
| MCP (Model Context Protocol) | A standard way for AI helpers to connect to outside tools. |
| OAuth | A login standard that grants a tool access without sharing your password. |
| Clone / digital twin | Your personal AI copy, trained on your writing to work in your style. |
| GDPR | The European Union's privacy law protecting personal data. |
| SOC 2 | An independent audit checking how a company protects customer data. |
