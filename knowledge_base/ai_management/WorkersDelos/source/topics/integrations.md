3000+ Integrations — Slack, Teams, Gmail | Workers Delos 
 More than 3000 integrations for your AI Workers 
 Your AI Workers connect natively to Slack, Microsoft Teams, Gmail, Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce and more than 3000 other tools. They work directly where your team already works, without forcing you to change your habits or adopt a new tool. 
 Setup takes less than 5 minutes, without writing a single line of code. You connect a tool in a few clicks via OAuth, define what the worker is allowed to read and write, and it starts working. No infrastructure to maintain on your side. 
 Communication and messaging: Slack, Microsoft Teams, Gmail, Outlook. Your AI Workers receive requests, reply, notify and report back directly in your channels. A worker can monitor a Slack channel, handle incoming requests and escalate to a human when needed. 
 Productivity and documentation: Notion, Google Drive, Confluence, Google Docs. Workers read your reference documents, write summaries, update knowledge bases and keep your documentation up to date automatically. 
 CRM and sales: HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive. A worker can enrich contact records, qualify leads, prepare email sequences and keep your pipeline clean without manual intervention. 
 Project management and development: Jira, Linear, GitHub, GitLab, Asana, Trello. Technical workers open tickets, comment on pull requests, track sprint progress and flag blockers to the team. 
 Every integration respects granular permissions: you decide, tool by tool, what the worker can see and do. You can revoke access at any time, and none of your data is used to train models. Connections use the vendors’ standard OAuth protocols, with no password storage. 
 If a tool you use isn’t on the list yet, most workflows can be connected via webhooks or API. Contact the Delos team to assess your specific integration need.
