Clone Yourself in AI — Digital Twin 
 Create your digital twin and clone yourself in AI 
 Train an AI clone that answers, works and reasons like you, keeping your style, tone and expertise. 
 Delegate your repetitive tasks to your digital twin and focus on what really matters. 
 The clone learns from your writing (emails, Slack messages, Notion documents, LinkedIn posts) to reproduce your voice faithfully. The more you interact with it, the more it refines its style. You can correct its suggestions and it takes them into account for the next interactions. 
 Typical use cases: replying to recurring emails on your behalf, preparing first drafts of documents, running your social media presence, managing the sorting and prioritization of your inbox, writing meeting notes in your tone. 
 The clone runs in an isolated environment dedicated to your organization. You define the scope of action: what it can do alone, what requires your approval, what is off-limits. You keep full control and can disable the clone at any time. 
 The clone can also learn from your reference documents (internal processes, templates, brand guidelines, decision archives). The more context it has about how you work, the more its output matches what you would have produced yourself. You can import these documents in a few clicks through your Notion, Google Drive or Confluence integrations. 
 Unlike a simple text autotune, the clone reasons about your preferences: it knows you prefer concise answers, that you always confirm in writing, that you start emails with a first name. These nuances make the difference between a credible clone and a generic assistant. The clone evolves with you: if your style changes, it adapts. 
 The clone is especially useful for executives and independents who must be present across several channels at once: replying to clients, running a blog, posting on LinkedIn, preparing presentations. Your digital twin absorbs the repetitive volume while you focus on high-value interactions. 
 Creating the clone is gradual: you start by giving it a simple task (sorting emails, writing summaries), you check the result, then you expand its scope. In a few weeks, the clone handles most of your repetitive tasks with enough fidelity that the people you deal with don’t notice the difference.
