 Frequently asked questions 
 How do I install Companion? 
 Find Delos on the Chrome Web Store or the Microsoft Edge Add-on. Once downloaded, open the extension from the puzzle icon in your browser toolbar, then sign in with your Delos account. 
 Is Companion available on Edge? 
 Yes, Companion is available on both Chrome and Microsoft Edge. Download the extension from the store that matches your browser. 
 What features does Companion offer? 
 Companion includes an email assistant (correction, translation, rewriting), chat with web pages using your preferred LLMs, live translation of selected text, and a writing assistant for fixing mistakes.
