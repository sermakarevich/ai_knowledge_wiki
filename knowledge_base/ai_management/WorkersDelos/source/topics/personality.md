AI Worker Personality | Workers Delos 
 AI Workers with a real personality 
 Our AI Workers are not generic chatbots: each has its own tone, communication style and specialties. 
 The result: real virtual colleagues who are pleasant and effective to work with every day. 
 James, our marketing worker, communicates with a results-driven tone and talks ROI before creativity. Laura, the developer, is precise and technical, cites PR numbers and proposes diffs. Sophie, the designer, thinks in visual systems and talks accessibility. Atif, the data analyst, structures everything into tables and metrics. 
 This personality is not cosmetic: it shapes how each worker approaches a task, phrases its feedback, and interacts with your team in Slack. A marketing worker doesn’t write a brief the way a developer worker writes a README. Profiles are calibrated to match the communication codes of their role. 
 You can also adjust a worker’s personality: more formal or more casual tone, level of detail in responses, notification frequency. The worker keeps these preferences thanks to its persistent memory and applies them to every interaction. 
 Beyond tone, workers also have specialties that guide their priorities. Nova, our SEO specialist, thinks Core Web Vitals and indexing before content. Karen, our customer success manager, anticipates churn risk and proposes retention plans. Ivan, the project manager, reasons in sprints, dependencies and blockers. These professional biases make workers more relevant than generic assistants. 
 Personality also shows in communication channels: a worker can chime in on Slack with a brief, direct tone, then write a more formal client email. The worker adapts its register to the context, like an experienced human colleague would. This flexibility is essential for a natural fit into your existing workflows.
