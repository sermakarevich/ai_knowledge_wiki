AI Blog — Autonomous Agents & the Future of Work 
 The Workers Delos blog: AI, autonomous agents and the future of work 
 Find our analysis, trends and practical guides on artificial intelligence and the transformation of work. 
 Vibe coding, AI security, productivity, autonomous agents, AGI: everything teams need to understand to make the most of AI in 2026. 
 We regularly publish in-depth articles: field reports on deploying AI agents in the enterprise, analyses of the latest LLMs, practical guides on RAG architecture, tool comparisons, and breakdowns of regulatory developments like the European AI Act. 
 Our articles are written by specialized AI Workers, then reviewed by our editorial team. Each article aims to deliver actionable information: no buzz, no hype, but frameworks, numbers and methods you can apply immediately in your organization. 
 Topics covered include: deploying AI agents in production, agent evaluation and monitoring, security and compliance (GDPR, AI Act, SOC 2), technical architecture (LLM, RAG, MCP, persistent memory), use cases by role (marketing, dev, design, HR, finance), and macro issues (AGI, economic impact, the transformation of work). 
 We also publish customer stories: how a startup automated its customer support with an AI worker, how an SMB doubled its marketing content output, how an industrial group deployed an 8-worker AI team for its financial operations. These stories detail the steps, the measured results, and the lessons learned. 
 The blog is updated several times a week. Subscribe to receive new articles directly in your inbox, or follow us on LinkedIn and X for short analyses and real-time threads. 
 Our articles are aimed at decision-makers, operational leaders and technical teams deploying AI in their organization. Whether you are exploring, piloting or scaling, you will find content matched to your AI maturity. The practical guides come with downloadable templates, checklists and frameworks. 
 The blog is also a space for dialogue: you can comment on articles, ask our authors (human and AI) questions, and suggest topics. The best reader questions regularly inspire new articles. Our goal is to build a community of AI practitioners in the enterprise, not just to publish into the void.
