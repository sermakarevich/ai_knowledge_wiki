# Retrieval Practice: Workers Delos — Autonomous AI Workers, 24/7

### Q1. What is a Workers Delos AI Worker, and which roles does it cover?

> [!tip]- Answer
> An AI Worker is an autonomous AI agent that works like a real teammate: it has its own personality, specialized skills, and persistent memory, and it runs complete tasks end to end, 24/7. It covers marketing, development, design, data, human resources (HR — the team that hires and supports people), finance, and customer support, with specialized profiles in the catalog. See [[wiki/01-hire-autonomous-ai-workers-that-work-like-real-teammates|Hire Autonomous AI Workers that work like real teammates]].

### Q2. Who builds Delos, and on what principles?

> [!tip]- Answer
> Delos is a European company building a secure, multimodal AI (artificial intelligence) workspace where AI applications, models, and autonomous agents act across the tools teams already use. Its stated principles are traceable automation with human validation on important decisions, so people stay in control of what agents do. See [[wiki/02-about|About Delos]].

### Q3. How do you build a team of AI Workers, and how does it scale?

> [!tip]- Answer
> You can assemble a custom team of 1 to 15 AI Workers covering marketing, development, design, support, finance, and more, instead of hiring people for each role. Capacity scales up or down instantly and the workers coordinate through shared memory, so the team grows without recruiting delays. See [[wiki/04-build-a-team|Build Your AI Team]].

### Q4. What is the "clone yourself" digital twin, and what stays under your control?

> [!tip]- Answer
> You can train an AI clone — a digital twin — that answers, works, and reasons like you in your own style, so repetitive tasks get delegated while output still sounds like you. You control its scope and approvals, deciding what it may handle alone and what needs your sign-off. See [[wiki/05-clone-yourself|Clone Yourself in AI]].

### Q5. What is Companion, and where does it work?

> [!tip]- Answer
> Companion is the Delos browser extension for Chrome and Edge that brings AI writing help, page chat with your favourite LLMs (large language models — the AI engines behind chatbots), and instant translation into every website you browse. It puts assistance directly into your inbox and daily browsing instead of a separate app. See [[wiki/06-compagnon|Companion — Browser Extension]].

### Q6. What gives each AI Worker its own personality?

> [!tip]- Answer
> Each AI Worker has a role-calibrated tone, style, and professional biases rather than a generic chatbot voice, so a finance analyst sounds different from a designer. You can adjust the personality and it is remembered across Slack, email, and every interaction. See [[wiki/08-personality|Worker Personality]].

### Q7. How do AI Workers connect to the tools a team already uses?

> [!tip]- Answer
> Workers connect natively to Slack, Microsoft Teams, Gmail, Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, and 3000+ other tools with no-code setup in under 5 minutes. They work where the team already works, under granular per-tool permissions that limit what each worker may access. See [[wiki/07-integrations|Integrations]].

### Q8. What technology powers the workers under the hood?

> [!tip]- Answer
> Workers are built on latest-generation language models plus RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation — a method where the AI looks up your documents before answering) and isolated per-tenant persistent memory. Their work is orchestrated through multi-agent workflows on open standards, with every action logged, permissioned, and open to human approval. See [[wiki/16-tech|Tech — LLM, RAG, Autonomous Agents]].

### Q9. How much does Delos cost, and is there a free option?

> [!tip]- Answer
> The Delos Office Suite sells four monthly plans: Student €10, Explore €25, Advanced €45, and Premium €80, plus per-Worker AI Worker hiring and a custom Enterprise plan for larger needs. You can start for free to test AI Workers first, and there is no minimum commitment — scale up, down, or cancel at any time. See [[wiki/09-pricing|Pricing]].

### Q10. How is customer data protected?

> [!tip]- Answer
> Workers Delos promises enterprise-grade protection that is GDPR-compliant (follows the EU privacy law) and SOC 2-certified (independently security-audited): end-to-end encryption, isolated environments, and granular permissions. Your data is never used to train models, and you keep full control over each worker's access. See [[wiki/10-security|Security & GDPR]].

### Q11. What do the role-specific solution agents actually do?

> [!tip]- Answer
> Each solution page describes a virtual specialist available 24/7: the design agent produces mockups and developer-ready specs, the developer agent writes code and reviews pull requests, the finance agent automates reporting and forecasts, the marketing agent runs SEO (Search Engine Optimization — improving pages so they rank higher in search), social, and email, and the HR agent screens applications and automates onboarding. See [[wiki/14-solutions-marketing|Solutions Marketing — Autonomous AI Marketing Agent]].

### Q16. (Evaluation) A five-person startup wants to try Delos with one worker first. What should they do, and is it a sensible move?

> [!tip]- Answer
> Yes, a one-worker trial is sensible: start for free with no commitment, pick the profile matching the biggest bottleneck (for example Nova for SEO or Laura for content), connect only the needed tools with tight permissions, and judge the documented time savings after a few weeks. If the worker proves itself, scale to a small team of complementary profiles; if not, remove it at no cost. See [[wiki/04-build-a-team|Build Your AI Team]].
