# Camille — Bid Manager

**In one sentence:** Camille is an autonomous AI Worker who works as a Bid Manager within your team, 24/7, with her own personality, skills and memory.

## Key points

- Camille works as a Bid Manager within your team, 24/7, with her own personality, skills and memory.
- Her key skills are Appels d'offres (calls for tender) and Qualification.
- She masters Notion and Slack natively, and integrates with Slack, Microsoft Teams and more than 3000 applications, with no code.
- Her voice is structured, analytical and collaborative: she centralises and qualifies tenders and frankly says whether to bid or not, backed by an evidenced go/no-go recommendation.
- She mobilises the right internal experts and pilots the tender dashboard from reception to result, learning from every win and loss.
- She collects incoming tenders, extracts key criteria and qualifies them on reception.
- She backs each go/no-go decision with a fit, win-chance and workload analysis, and identifies and calls on the right experts for a solid response.
- Hiring Camille delegates Bid Manager tasks and frees the team from repetitive work; add or remove an AI Worker at any time, with no commitment.
