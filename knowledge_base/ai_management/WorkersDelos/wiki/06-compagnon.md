# Companion — Browser Extension
**In one sentence:** Companion is the Delos browser extension for Chrome and Edge that puts AI (artificial intelligence) writing help, page chat with your favourite LLMs (large language models), and instant translation directly into your inbox and every website you browse.
## Key points
- Companion brings Delos into the browser across all websites and directly into emails; it is available on the Chrome Web Store and the Microsoft Edge Add-on.
- The page positions it as more than a simple extension: a secure, intuitive, powerful daily-productivity ally with professional-grade AI features in the browser.
- Email tools without leaving the inbox: your own correction, translation, and rewrite buttons, plus a dedicated writing-assistance interface for fixing mistakes ergonomically.
- Chat directly with your favourite LLMs while querying the pages you are browsing.
- Instant translation: select any text, request the translation, and get the result immediately in the same window.
- Installation: download from the store matching your browser, open the extension from the puzzle icon in the toolbar, then sign in with your Delos account; available on both Chrome and Edge.

## FAQ
- **How do I install Companion?** "Find Delos on the Chrome Web Store or the Microsoft Edge Add-on. Once downloaded, open the extension from the puzzle icon in your browser toolbar, then sign in with your Delos account."
- **Is Companion available on Edge?** "Yes, Companion is available on both Chrome and Microsoft Edge."
