# Solutions RH — Autonomous AI HR Agent

**In one sentence:** The AI HR (Human Resources — the team that hires and supports people) agent is a virtual HR assistant available 24/7 that screens applications, schedules interviews, automates onboarding, answers employee questions, and keeps HR metrics up to date.

## Key points

- The AI HR agent screens applications and pre-screens resumes.
- It schedules and coordinates interviews.
- It handles onboarding and automates HR processes.
- It answers employee questions, including recurring HR questions.
- It keeps HR metrics up to date and produces HR reporting.
- Named integrations: Slack, Gmail, Notion, Google Calendar, Lever, BambooHR — plus more than 3000 tools, with no code.
