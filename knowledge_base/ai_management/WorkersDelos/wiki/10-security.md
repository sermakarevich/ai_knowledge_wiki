# Security & GDPR
**In one sentence:** Workers Delos promises enterprise-grade, GDPR-compliant (follows the EU privacy law) and SOC 2-certified (independently security-audited) protection — end-to-end encryption, isolated environments, granular permissions — with your data never used to train models.
## Key points
- Data is protected by end-to-end encryption, isolated environments, and granular permissions.
- Workers Delos is GDPR-compliant and SOC 2-certified.
- None of your data is used to train models.
- You keep full control over what AI Workers can see and do.
- The page is thin by design — guarantees plus two FAQs — so worker capabilities and integration mechanics live on the richer neighbours (homepage wiki/01, integrations, build-a-team).
- FAQ confirms: GDPR-compliant and SOC 2-certified, data encrypted end to end and hosted in isolated environments.
- FAQ confirms: no training on your data, with full user control over worker visibility and actions.
