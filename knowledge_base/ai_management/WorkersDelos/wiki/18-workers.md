# Workers — 15 Specialized AI Workers

**In one sentence:** The catalog offers 15 autonomous AI agents across marketing, development, design, data, HR, finance, and customer support — each with its own tone, profile, skills, tools, and documented time savings — plus the option to clone yourself as a digital twin on a shared LLM-plus-memory-plus-RAG architecture.

## Key points

- The catalog holds 15 specialized AI Workers covering marketing, development, design, data, HR (Human Resources), finance, and customer support.
- Named workers include James in marketing, Laura in development, Sophie in design, and Atif in data, each with a detailed profile, precise skills, and concrete use cases.
- Workers are not simple chatbots: each has its own tone, communication style, and specialties (James is analytical and results-driven; Laura codes to your conventions and reviews PRs; Sophie adapts visual identity across formats).
- Every profile details key skills, mastered tools (Slack, GitHub, Figma, HubSpot, Jira, Notion, Salesforce), documented use cases, and expected time savings.
- You can compare workers and assemble a full AI team covering all business needs; hiring takes minutes.
- Beyond the catalog, you can create an AI clone of yourself — a digital twin that answers, works, and reasons like you, keeping your style, tone, and expertise.
- All workers share one technical architecture: latest-generation language models, persistent memory, and RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) knowledge access, learning from feedback while keeping data protected.
- The catalog is regularly enriched with new profiles, and existing workers receive skill and integration updates as the AI ecosystem evolves, with no update management on your side.
