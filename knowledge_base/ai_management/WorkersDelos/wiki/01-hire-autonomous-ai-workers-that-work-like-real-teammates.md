# Hire Autonomous AI Workers that work like real teammates

**In one sentence:** Workers Delos lets you hire specialized autonomous AI workers — with their own personality, skills, and memory — that plug into Slack, Teams, and 3000+ tools and run complete tasks end to end, 24/7.

## Key points

- Workers Delos offers specialized AI workers for marketing, development, design, data, human resources (HR — the team that hires and supports people), finance, and customer support.
- Each AI Worker has its own personality, specialized skills, and persistent memory of your preferences and interaction history.
- Workers integrate with Slack, Microsoft Teams, and more than 3000 tools, including Gmail, Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, Jira, GitHub, and Figma.
- Over 10,000 workers are already deployed, and 15 specialized AI Worker profiles are available in the catalog.
- Unlike generic chatbots, the workers slot into existing business processes, access your data securely through RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation — a method where the AI looks up your documents before answering), and learn from your feedback.
- Hiring takes less than five minutes: pick a profile from the catalog, connect your tools, describe the missions to hand off, and the worker starts executing immediately.
- Security: data is encrypted end to end, hosted in isolated environments, never used to train models; the service is GDPR-compliant (follows EU privacy rules) and SOC 2-certified (an independent security audit standard).
- Trying it is commitment-free: a free trial is included, you can add or remove workers at any time, and you keep full control over each worker's permissions and access.
