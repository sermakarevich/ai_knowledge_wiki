# Clone Yourself in AI
**In one sentence:** You can train an AI (artificial intelligence) clone — a digital twin — that answers, works, and reasons like you in your own style, delegating repetitive tasks while you control its scope and approvals.
## Key points
- The clone learns from your writing — emails, Slack messages, Notion documents, LinkedIn posts — to reproduce your voice, refining its style the more you interact with it and accepting your corrections.
- Typical uses: replying to recurring emails, preparing first document drafts, running social media presence, sorting and prioritizing the inbox, writing meeting notes in your tone.
- The clone runs in an isolated environment dedicated to your organization; you define what it can do alone, what needs approval, what is off-limits, and you can disable it at any time.
- It can also learn from reference documents — internal processes, templates, brand guidelines, decision archives — imported in a few clicks via Notion, Google Drive, or Confluence integrations.
- Unlike simple text autotune, the clone reasons about preferences: concise answers, always confirming in writing, starting emails with a first name — and it adapts when your style changes.
- It suits executives and independents who must be present on several channels at once: clients, blog, LinkedIn, presentations — the twin absorbs repetitive volume while you keep high-value interactions.
- Rollout is gradual: start with one simple task (sorting emails, writing summaries), check results, then expand scope until most repetitive tasks are handled in a few weeks.
