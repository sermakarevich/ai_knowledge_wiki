# Jeanne — Comptable

**In one sentence:** Jeanne is an autonomous AI Worker who works as a Comptable within your team, 24/7, with her own personality, skills and memory.

## Key points

- Jeanne works as a Comptable within your team, 24/7, with her own personality, skills and memory.
- Her key skills are Finance and Rapprochement (reconciliation).
- She masters Qonto and Pennylane natively, and integrates with Slack, Microsoft Teams and more than 3000 applications, with no code.
- Her voice is precise, organised and reliable: numbers never lie and she keeps them exact, chasing colleagues for missing receipts and centralising everything in one place.

- She reconciles every expense against its bank transaction and receipt, flagging gaps immediately.
- Every Monday she delivers a clear weekly financial report with a real debrief: spending, gaps, points of attention.
- She analyses each reimbursement request, checks compliance and formulates an approval recommendation; hiring Jeanne delegates Comptable tasks with no commitment, add or remove at any time.
