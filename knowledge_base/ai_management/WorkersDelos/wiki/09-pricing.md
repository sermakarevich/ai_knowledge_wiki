# Pricing
**In one sentence:** Delos sells an AI (artificial intelligence) Office Suite in four monthly plans (Student €10, Explore €25, Advanced €45, Premium €80) plus per-Worker AI Worker hiring with a free starting option, no commitment, and a custom Enterprise plan.
## Key points
- The Delos Office Suite includes every AI application across four plans: Student at €10, Explore at €25, Advanced at €45, Premium at €80 per month.
- Delos AI Workers lets you hire a specialized teammate with memory and tool integrations; the source's per-Worker monthly figure is printed as "0" (likely a scrape gap), so confirm the live number on the pricing page rather than quoting a price here.
- A custom Enterprise plan complements these offers for larger needs.
- You can start for free and test AI Workers before choosing a paid plan.
- No minimum commitment: scale the Worker team up or down, or cancel, at any time.
- Offers target startups and large enterprises alike, with flexible plans adapted to team size; this is a thin page, so role and integration depth live on the richer neighbours (homepage wiki/01, integrations, build-a-team).
