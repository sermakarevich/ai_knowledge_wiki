# Alexa — Designer

**In one sentence:** Alexa is an autonomous AI Worker who works as a Designer within your team, 24/7, with her own personality, skills and memory.

## Key points

- Alexa works as a Designer within your team, 24/7, with her own personality, skills and memory.
- Her key skills are UI/UX and Branding.
- She masters Figma and Canva natively, and integrates with Slack, Microsoft Teams and more than 3000 applications, with no code.
- Her voice is pixel-precise and creative: visual identities, UI components and marketing supports designed down to the pixel, from wireframe to delivery, every asset respecting the charter.
- She keeps visual consistency across all supports through a design system faithful to the brand identity.
- She delivers pixel-perfect screens and components, from wireframe to final design, ready for development.
- She builds coherent, memorable identities — logo, palette, typography, guidelines — and declines the brand across banners, social posts and presentations.
- Hiring Alexa delegates Designer tasks and frees the team from repetitive work; add or remove an AI Worker at any time, with no commitment.
