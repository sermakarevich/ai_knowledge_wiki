# Build Your AI Team
**In one sentence:** You can assemble a custom team of 1 to 15 AI Workers covering marketing, development, design, support, finance, and more, scaling capacity up or down instantly with shared memory and coordination instead of hiring.
## Key points
- Assemble a custom AI Worker team by combining profiles you need — marketing, development, design, support, finance — from 1 up to 15 workers.
- Scaling is instant with no hiring process: add a worker during spikes (product launch, high season, fundraising) and remove it when volume drops, with no notice period or admin overhead.
- A complete team covers the value chain: marketing attracts visitors, SEO (search engine optimization) optimizes visibility, development builds the product, design keeps visual identity, support handles customers, finance tracks cash flow.
- Workers share context through persistent memory and connected integrations, so the marketing worker sees support tickets and finance receives sales signals — native coordination that avoids silos.
- Building an AI team augments rather than replaces staff: workers absorb repetitive, predictable volume while humans keep strategy, creativity, and complex relationships.
- The proven path: start with one worker on a high-impact use case, measure results, then add complementary profiles until the value chain is covered in a few weeks.

## Shared context and coordination
Workers share a common context through persistent memory and connected integrations: the marketing worker sees tickets opened by the support worker, the finance worker receives signals from the sales worker. Each worker knows what the others are doing and coordinates accordingly, reproducing the dynamics of a real team.

## Augmentation, not substitution, and the rollout path
Workers handle repetitive, predictable volume, freeing teams for high-value work (strategy, creativity, complex relationships); they integrate into the existing organization and complement headcount without replacing it. Deployment path: start with one worker on a high-impact use case, measure results, then add complementary profiles — full value-chain coverage in a few weeks.
