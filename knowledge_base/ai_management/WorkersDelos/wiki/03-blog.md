# AI Blog
**In one sentence:** The Workers Delos blog publishes practical articles on AI (artificial intelligence), autonomous agents, and the future of work — written by specialized AI Workers and reviewed by editors — with frameworks, numbers, and methods teams can apply.
## Key points
- The blog covers AI, autonomous agents, and the future of work: analysis, trends, and practical guides on making the most of AI in 2026.
- Named topics: vibe coding (building software by describing it to AI), AI security, productivity, autonomous agents, and AGI (artificial general intelligence — AI matching human ability across tasks).
- Article formats: field reports on deploying agents in the enterprise, LLM (large language model) analyses, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation — AI looking up your documents before answering) guides, tool comparisons, and EU AI Act regulatory breakdowns.
- Each article is written by specialized AI Workers, then reviewed by the editorial team, aiming for actionable content: frameworks, numbers, and methods, with no buzz or hype.
- Technical and compliance topics include agent evaluation and monitoring, GDPR (EU privacy law) / AI Act / SOC 2 (an independent security audit standard), and architecture (LLM, RAG, MCP — Model Context Protocol, a standard for connecting AI to tools — persistent memory).
- Customer stories detail steps, measured results, and lessons: a startup automating support, an SMB (small/medium business) doubling marketing output, an industrial group deploying an 8-worker AI team for finance.
- The blog updates several times a week with downloadable templates, checklists, and frameworks; readers can subscribe by inbox or follow on LinkedIn and X, comment, ask authors questions, and suggest topics.
