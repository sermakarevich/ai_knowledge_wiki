# AI Management

Research on **how management, teams, and organizations should operate in the AI era** — workforce design, team coordination, organizational workflow redesign, and leadership practices for AI-first environments.

## Papers

- [[AiIsEverywhereAgenticOrganization/summary]] — Over 80% of companies see no AI ROI from point solutions; real gains require end-to-end workflow redesign with humans "above the loop."
- [[DesigningTechWorkforceForAiFirstEra/summary]] — CIOs must hire fewer but more senior engineers, reskill staff, and move vendor contracts from labor- to outcome-based.
- [[ReinventingMarketingWorkflowsWithAgenticAI/summary]] — Agentic AI can automate ~60% of marketing tasks and accelerate campaign execution 10–15x; bottleneck is workflow redesign, not model capability.
- [[ZeroAlignment/summary]] — AI coding agents accelerate individual output but destroy team alignment; Ace multiplayer workspace restores shared coordination at agentic velocity.
