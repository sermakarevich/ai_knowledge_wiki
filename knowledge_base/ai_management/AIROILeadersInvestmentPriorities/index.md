# How AI ROI Leaders prioritize investments for real business outcomes

A Google Cloud Blog article by Oliver Parker (VP, Global Generative AI GTM) presenting the third annual "ROI of AI" survey, run with the National Research Group across 2,403 executives in global markets and all industries. Its central claim is a management claim rather than a technical one: 84% of executives report increasing financial returns from AI, but the 26% whose returns are *accelerating* -- the "AI ROI Leaders" -- are distinguished by three organizational practices (clear ownership and decision rights, AI embedded into core business processes, mandated ongoing AI fluency), with agents named as the catalyst that converts those practices into revenue and cost outcomes.

It is worth ingesting as the most quantified statement of the current enterprise-AI-returns consensus, and as a case study in what vendor survey evidence can and cannot establish -- the underlying distribution charts are published, which makes several of the headline percentages checkable against their own data.
