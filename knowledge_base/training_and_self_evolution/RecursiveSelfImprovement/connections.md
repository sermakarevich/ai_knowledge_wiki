# Connections

- [[SelfRefine/summary|Self-Refine: Iterative Refinement with Self-Feedback]] — This is the canonical example of this survey's B0 baseline (non-RSI): Self-Refine's generate→feedback→refine loop improves only the current task output and discards everything afterward, with no persistent system change — exactly the "retry, then forget" rung the survey excludes from RSI, and its Math Reasoning failure (the model can't spot its own errors, 94% "everything looks good") previews the survey's "reliable verification" credibility check at a larger scale.
- [[SelfHarness/summary|Self-Harness: Harnesses That Improve Themselves]] — A concrete L2 system in this survey's own taxonomy: it mines execution-trace weaknesses, proposes and tests harness edits, and keeps only validated changes under a fixed benchmark and promotion rule — objective and acceptance criteria stay human-set (L2's defining boundary), while the AI autonomously chooses *how* to improve, matching the survey's L2 loop (observe → diagnose → select how to change → test → retain or revert) almost verbatim.
- [[RedQueenGodelMachine/summary|The Red Queen Gödel Machine: Co-Evolving Agents and Their Evaluators]] — Directly cited inside this survey as the paper's own worked example of the "reliable verification" credibility check: it freezes evaluators within each epoch and validates replacements against an independent ground-truth anchor specifically to prevent the evaluator-gaming failure mode (cherry-picked seeds, exploited judges) that the survey warns is endemic to self-improving loops that let the grader and the student co-evolve unchecked.
- [[PrimeAgentSelfImprovingHarness/summary|Prime Agent: A Self-Improving RLM Harness]] — A candidate higher-rung system worth placing on the survey's ladder: its persistent, self-improving substrate (L0-L3 caches, recursive subagents, Continual Harness) that raises ARC-AGI-3 Best@1 from 30% to 95.5% looks like it reaches at least L2-L3 (the system revises its own state and possibly its acquisition strategy), but per this survey's own "structural vs. effective L5" distinction, the open question is whether its harness-level improvements ever feed back into revising the *mechanism* that generates future harness revisions, or whether the harness itself stays a fixed, human-designed structure.
- [[SelfRevisingDiscoverySystems/summary|Self-Revising Discovery Systems for Science]] — Complementary rather than overlapping: this survey's science regime (S1) asks whether AI can evolve hypothesis modules, experimental agents, and reflection mechanisms within a fixed scientific framework (reaching L2 at best), while Self-Revising Discovery Systems goes one level further conceptually — proposing a categorical framework for AI that recognizes when the scientific framework *itself* is broken and needs to be replaced, which is closer to this survey's L5 (revising the mechanism that governs future improvement) applied to the practice of science rather than to model training.
