# Gen AI's next inflection point — wiki hub

McKinsey's August 2024 transformation brief: employees adopted gen AI first (91% use it for work) while organizations stalled at pilots (13% early adopters), so capturing value demands domain-led operating-model reinvention, role-by-role reskilling, and governance-grade reinforcement.
