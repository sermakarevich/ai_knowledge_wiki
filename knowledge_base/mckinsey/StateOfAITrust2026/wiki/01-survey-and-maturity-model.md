# Survey design and the AI Trust Maturity Model

**In one sentence:** McKinsey's 2026 AI Trust Maturity Survey of about 500 organizations scores responsible Artificial Intelligence practices on a 0–4 scale across five dimensions, and finds overall maturity rising even as strategy, governance, and agentic controls lag.

## Key points

- The survey ran December 2025–January 2026 across roughly 500 organizations, with respondents holding direct responsibility or expertise in Artificial Intelligence governance, risk management, or Artificial Intelligence investment decisions.
- Responses are scored with the McKinsey Artificial Intelligence Trust Maturity Model across five dimensions of responsible Artificial Intelligence: strategy, risk management, data and technology, governance, and agentic Artificial Intelligence governance and controls, the last new this year.
- Maturity is ranked across four levels, from developing foundational practices to running a comprehensive and proactive program, on a 0–4 scale.
- The average maturity score rose to 2.3 in 2026 from 2.0 in 2025, a 0.3-point gain that still leaves the typical organization mid-journey rather than mature.
- Only about 30 percent of organizations reach level 3 or higher in strategy, governance, and agentic Artificial Intelligence governance, the three dimensions that provide alignment and oversight.
- The article frames trust as serving two outcomes: unlocking value from Artificial Intelligence investments through sustained adoption, and managing an expanding risk landscape as systems gain autonomy.
- The agentic shift reframes the trust problem from systems saying the wrong thing to systems doing the wrong thing, such as unintended actions, tool misuse, or operating beyond guardrails.

---

## Survey design

### Who was asked and when

The survey was taken between December 2025 and January 2026 and gathered responses from approximately 500 organizations across industries and regions. Respondents were people with direct responsibility or expertise in Artificial Intelligence governance, risk management, or Artificial Intelligence investment decisions, so the results reflect practitioner and decision-maker views rather than general employee sentiment.

### The five-dimension model

The McKinsey Artificial Intelligence Trust Maturity Model assesses five dimensions: strategy, risk management, data and technology, governance, plus agentic Artificial Intelligence governance and controls, added in 2026 "reflecting the growing importance of governing increasingly autonomous Artificial Intelligence systems". Maturity runs across four levels, "from the development of foundational responsible Artificial Intelligence practices to the implementation of a comprehensive and proactive program".

## Overall maturity result

### The headline numbers

The average responsible Artificial Intelligence maturity score increased to 2.3 in 2026, up from 2.0 in 2025. But only about one-third of organizations report maturity levels of three or higher in strategy, governance, and agentic Artificial Intelligence governance. The article's diagnosis: "while technical and risk management capabilities are advancing, organizational alignment and oversight structures are struggling to keep pace with the rapid expansion of Artificial Intelligence use."

### Why trust is framed as foundational

The article argues Artificial Intelligence trust and the responsible practices behind it "are no longer a tangential concern but a foundational requirement for realizing the full potential of the technology". Trust underpins two outcomes: realizing value from Artificial Intelligence investments "by supporting sustained adoption and integration into core workflows", and managing "an expanding and evolving risk landscape". The agentic-era formulation is explicit: organizations "must also contend with systems doing the wrong thing, such as taking unintended actions, misusing tools, or operating beyond appropriate guardrails".
