# Maturity by industry, region, and investment

**In one sentence:** Technology, media, and telecom plus financial services lead on maturity, Asia–Pacific leads across regions, and organizations investing 25 million dollars or more in responsible Artificial Intelligence report markedly higher maturity and material business value.

## Key points

- Technology, media, and telecommunications and financial services lead in maturity, driven by stronger risk management and data foundations.
- Asia–Pacific leads overall maturity across regions, while governance and agentic Artificial Intelligence controls trail data and technology in every region, a globally consistent governance gap.
- Organizations investing 25 million dollars or more in responsible Artificial Intelligence initiatives report significantly higher maturity scores than lower-spending peers.
- Those large investors are far more likely to realize material Artificial Intelligence benefits, including earnings before interest and taxes impact above 5 percent.
- The article presents this as evidence that responsible Artificial Intelligence spending enables rather than taxes innovation, calling it "a key enabler of sustained value creation".
- The regional pattern implies the governance shortfall is structural and worldwide, not a quirk of one market's regulation or culture.
- Industry leadership by regulated and data-heavy sectors suggests prior risk-management muscle transfers directly into Artificial Intelligence trust maturity.

---

## Industry differences

### Who leads and why

Technology, media, and telecommunications and financial services "continue to lead in responsible Artificial Intelligence maturity, driven by stronger risk management and data foundations". The mechanism is intuitive: sectors that already run formal risk functions and large governed data estates can extend those foundations to Artificial Intelligence, while sectors without them must build both at once.

## Regional differences

### Asia–Pacific on top, governance gap everywhere

"Regionally, Asia–Pacific leads overall maturity, while governance and agentic Artificial Intelligence controls lag behind data and technology across all regions, indicating a globally consistent governance gap." Every region shows the same shape — technology capability ahead, oversight behind — which points to a structural feature of how fast deployment outruns governance rather than a regional policy difference.

## Investment and value

### The 25-million-dollar line

"Organizations investing $25 million or more into responsible Artificial Intelligence initiatives report significantly higher maturity scores and are far more likely to realize material Artificial Intelligence benefits, including earnings before interest and taxes impact above 5 percent." The article draws the causal-suggestive conclusion: "This relationship reinforces that responsible Artificial Intelligence investment is not a tax on innovation but a key enabler of sustained value creation." Note the design limit: the survey shows association between spending, maturity, and value, not proof that spending alone causes the gains — large committed investors likely differ in leadership attention and program quality too.
