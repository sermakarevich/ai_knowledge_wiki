# Unleashing developer productivity with generative AI

McKinsey's June 2023 lab study of 40-plus developers found AI assistants roughly double coding speed on standard tasks without hurting quality, while showing gains fade on complex work and reverse for juniors — and prescribing training, broader use cases, reskilling, and risk governance to capture the value.
