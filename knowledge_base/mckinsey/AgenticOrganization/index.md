# AI is everywhere. The agentic organization isn't — yet

McKinsey's Aneesh Rahilly interviews Bryan Krivkovich (People & Organizational Performance Practice) on why more than 80 percent of companies invest heavily in AI without seeing bottom-line impact. Krivkovich's answer: redesign five pillars together — the business model (near-zero marginal cost, hypersegmentation to the "unit of one"), team workflows (superhuman capabilities need new oversight and governance, not just new tools), org structure (flatter spans, domain-specific shapes), leadership (spend real time differently, not dabbling), and talent/culture (move humans above-the-loop, reskill instead of replace, run reversible two-way-door experiments). The throughline: point solutions don't capture value — only reimagined end-to-end workflows do, and the hardest open problem is preserving the junior-talent pipeline once entry-level work gets automated.
