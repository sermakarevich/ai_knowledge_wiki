# The State of AI in 2026: On the Road to ROI

McKinsey QuantumBlack's ninth annual global survey on corporate AI adoption (August 25, 2026). It tracks how far organizations have moved from experimenting with AI to scaling it enterprise-wide, and — its central tension — why individual employees report clear personal benefit while company-level financial impact has barely budged year over year. It was worth ingesting because it's the most-cited annual benchmark for enterprise AI adoption, cost, and workforce-impact trends, useful for calibrating expectations about agentic AI's real-world uptake.
