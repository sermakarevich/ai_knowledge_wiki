# The gen AI skills revolution: rethinking your talent strategy

Hussin, Wiesinger, Relyea, Harrysson, and Thareja's McKinsey Quarterly article (August 29, 2024) argues that generative Artificial Intelligence (gen AI) automates tasks rather than whole roles, so software organizations must rebuild talent strategy around skills: engineers shift from writing code to reviewing and designing, product managers carry an equal upskilling burden plus trust stewardship, roles absorb and merge while safety and operations specialties emerge, leaders standardize tooling and govern risk, and personnel teams maintain a living skills inventory taught through apprenticeship. Worth ingesting as the strategy companion to the knowledge base's workflow-redesign and code-review literature, and as the top-down complement to the HumanSideOfGenAI worker survey.
