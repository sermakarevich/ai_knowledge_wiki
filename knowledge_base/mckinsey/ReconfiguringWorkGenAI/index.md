# Reconfiguring work: change management in the age of gen AI

Erik Roth's McKinsey article (August 13, 2025) argues that piloting generative artificial intelligence is easy but creating value is hard — and that value comes from reconfiguring work around Gen AI with employees as co-creators, not from installing another tool. Its five-step CEO playbook (outcome-based North Star, trust through data and governance, workflow redesign toward AI teams, MVO-plus-augmented-team structures, mass employee participation) is grounded in McKinsey's own Lilli rollout and Singtel's 10,000-person academy. Worth ingesting as the practitioner change-management companion to the KB's more technical agentic-organization literature.
