# The human side of generative AI: creating a path to productivity

Aaron De Smet, Sandra Durth, Bryan Hancock, Marino Mugayar-Baldocchi, and Angelika Reich's McKinsey Quarterly article (March 18, 2024) argues that generative Artificial Intelligence productivity runs through people, not around them: a 12,802-worker Talent Trends Survey shows the gen AI vanguard is overwhelmingly nontechnical, flight-prone, motivated by flexibility and belonging rather than pay, and hungry for judgment skills over tool skills. Leaders who redesign jobs around human strengths — deciding what stays human, measuring outcomes instead of hours, and keeping a live dialogue — convert efficiency gains into loyalty and lasting performance. Worth ingesting as the people-side companion to the KB's workflow-redesign and change-management literature.
