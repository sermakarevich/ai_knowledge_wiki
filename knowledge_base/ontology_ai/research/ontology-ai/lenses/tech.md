# ontology_ai -- for software engineers building ontology-backed systems

> Lens over [[../digest|ontology_ai digest]] — same facts, selected and framed
> for engineers shipping systems backed by ontologies and knowledge graphs.

## What this means for you

- **Ontology work is a lifecycle, not a one-shot generation task.** The literature covers four stages — requirements specification, implementation, publication, and maintenance — with LLMs tried at every stage as drafter, domain expert, and evaluator ([[../../../LargeLanguageModelsForOntologyEngineering/summary|SLR]]). Plan your system for LLM assistance at each stage, with a human gate before anything is committed.
- **Expect a vocabulary-to-structure collapse from raw LLMs.** Nine LLMs recover terms at F1 ~59–67% (classes ahead of properties) but fall to ~32% property F1, ~12–15% global triple/axiom F1, ~17% hierarchy-closure F1, and ~2% full axiom coverage ([[../../../CQ4OE/summary|CQ4OE]]). A 6,370-article legal pipeline reached 100% JSON validity and ~99.98% class coverage but only 50–73% signature compliance from typing errors and novel domain–range reuse ([[../../../FrenchLegalKnowledgeGraph/summary|FrenchLegalKG]]). Never let unconstrained LLM output write directly to your ontology or graph.
- **The reliable NL-to-query pattern is full OWL in context, one deterministic call.** A single zero-shot call combining SPARQL instructions, the complete domain OWL ontology, a small domain-specific rider, and the user question — output stripped of fences and reasoning traces before execution, no fine-tuning, few-shot examples, or entity linking — reaches 89.9% Match / 81.3% Exact on 1,000 revised DBLP-QuAD 3.1 questions, 98/100 on SemOpenAlex (vs a published 86/100 baseline), and 100% on 21 neuroimaging competency questions ([[../../../NaturalLanguageKnowledgeGraphQueryExecution/summary|NLKGQ]]).
- **How the ontology reaches the model matters more than which model.** Formally typed ontology tokens (explicit domain/range, `rdfs:subClassOf` hierarchy, `owl:ObjectProperty` vs `owl:DatatypeProperty`) map directly onto SPARQL patterns; the two strongest dense models converge near 90% Match, and the stated hierarchy is formal concept transfer above architecture above scale above prompt engineering ([[../../../NaturalLanguageKnowledgeGraphQueryExecution/summary|NLKGQ]]). Readable names plus `rdfs:comment`/`rdfs:label` and explicit domain/range carry zero-shot SPARQL to 100% on a 21-question MRI benchmark vs 57% for SQL auto-generated from the same ontology, with an ablation spread from 100% (full Turtle) down to 5–19% (anonymised abstract forms) while prompts and temperatures matter little ([[../../../NaturalLanguageAccessToDomainSpecificMetadata/summary|NLAccessMetadata]]). Structured MCP/SPARQL tool access (F1 0.717) far exceeds dumping the raw OWL file into context (0.323), which fails systematically on domain/range triples ([[../../../OpenOntologies/summary|OpenOntologies]]).
- **Scale past context limits with retrieval, not bigger prompts.** A FAISS index over ontology elements plus top-k Turtle context and reuse-without-redeclaration prompting answers all 39 test CQs with 0% EU / 2.5% industry syntax errors, no new critical OOPS! pitfalls, and <2% superfluous elements ([[../../../OntoExtend/summary|OntoExtend]]). A production extraction layer with live Neo4j content-conditioned ontology retrieval cuts catalog tokens ~11,200 → ~700 (~94%) ([[../../../OntologyGuidedDeduplicationAwareExtractionLayer/summary|OntoGuidedDedup]]).
- **Handle opaque endpoints with wrapper ontologies, not endpoint rewrites.** A clean, readable, typed namespace is shown to the model and deterministically rewritten to native predicates before execution — federating DBLP plus OpenCitations, flattening SemOpenAlex intermediaries — without modifying the endpoint; the wrapper alone contributes ~25 Exact-match points on DBLP ([[../../../NaturalLanguageKnowledgeGraphQueryExecution/summary|NLKGQ]]).
- **Make LLM output merge-safe with deterministic layers.** Six deterministic zero-inference dedup algorithms plus an embedding resolver under a non-overridable hard-conflict guard lift search recall ~70% → ~95% with zero false merges; per-page PDF routing plus a five-stage clean/merge/second-pass pipeline cuts hallucinations from 174 to zero on an adversarial image-only corpus ([[../../../OntologyGuidedDeduplicationAwareExtractionLayer/summary|OntoGuidedDedup]]). Stable 1-to-1 matching lifts OAEI Anatomy alignment from F1 0.182 to 0.832 at record precision 0.963, while signal-weight tuning moves F1 by <0.004 ([[../../../OpenOntologies/summary|OpenOntologies]]).
- **Close the loop with symbolic validation plus human review.** SHACL/RDFLib syntax gates, OOPS! + Pellet/HermiT consistency checks, qualifier-preserving relationship folding, novel-type/predicate flagging for admin review, and engineer CQ-verification with correctness/completeness ratings are what turn fluent LLM drafts into committable changes ([[../../../OntoExtend/summary|OntoExtend]], [[../../../OpenOntologies/summary|OpenOntologies]], [[../../../FrenchLegalKnowledgeGraph/summary|FrenchLegalKG]], [[../../../OntologyGuidedDeduplicationAwareExtractionLayer/summary|OntoGuidedDedup]]).

## Decisions this informs

- **Query interface: full-ontology-in-context SPARQL vs alternatives.** Prefer putting the complete typed OWL ontology in context with a single deterministic zero-shot call over informal schema dumps, fine-tuning, agentic exploration, or retrieval pipelines; SPARQL substantially outperforms SQL on the same ontology and data ([[../../../NaturalLanguageKnowledgeGraphQueryExecution/summary|NLKGQ]], [[../../../NaturalLanguageAccessToDomainSpecificMetadata/summary|NLAccessMetadata]]).
- **Ontology authoring conventions for LLM consumption.** Invest in readable names, `rdfs:comment`/`rdfs:label` annotations, explicit domain/range, declared class hierarchy, and reuse-without-redeclaration prompting — these dominate model choice and prompt tuning ([[../../../NaturalLanguageAccessToDomainSpecificMetadata/summary|NLAccessMetadata]], [[../../../OntoExtend/summary|OntoExtend]]).
- **Tool access vs context dumps.** Expose the ontology through structured tool (MCP/SPARQL) access rather than dumping raw OWL files into context; enforce assignment constraints such as stable 1-to-1 matching instead of tuning signal weights ([[../../../OpenOntologies/summary|OpenOntologies]]).
- **Opaque or federated vocabularies: wrapper + deterministic rewrite.** Build a clean typed wrapper namespace for the model and rewrite to native predicates before execution instead of modifying endpoints or prose-patching the rider ([[../../../NaturalLanguageKnowledgeGraphQueryExecution/summary|NLKGQ]]).
- **Scaling strategy: per-CQ and content-conditioned retrieval.** Use FAISS-indexed ontology-element retrieval with top-k Turtle context per competency question, or live content-conditioned slice retrieval with token budgets, rather than growing the prompt ([[../../../OntoExtend/summary|OntoExtend]], [[../../../OntologyGuidedDeduplicationAwareExtractionLayer/summary|OntoGuidedDedup]]).
- **Validation and merge gates.** Gate every LLM-produced change behind SHACL/RDFLib syntax checks, OOPS!/Pellet/HermiT consistency checks, CQ-as-SPARQL verification, novel-predicate flagging for admin review, and engineer correctness/completeness ratings ([[../../../OntoExtend/summary|OntoExtend]], [[../../../OpenOntologies/summary|OpenOntologies]], [[../../../FrenchLegalKnowledgeGraph/summary|FrenchLegalKG]], [[../../../OntologyGuidedDeduplicationAwareExtractionLayer/summary|OntoGuidedDedup]]).
- **Evaluation: CQ-driven functional checks over lexical overlap.** Evaluate with CQ-as-SPARQL verification, axiom/term coverage, and deterministic references (the QuAD 3.1 revision with per-case repair logs), and distrust headline scores from benchmarks with non-deterministic references ([[../../../NaturalLanguageKnowledgeGraphQueryExecution/summary|NLKGQ]], [[../../../CQ4OE/summary|CQ4OE]]).

## Risks and unknowns

- **Evidence is fragmented and hard to compare.** Task definitions, datasets, metrics, and experimental workflows differ study to study across the 41 reviewed studies; several primaries omit complete evaluation protocols or release no code; only a small subset (four per the second review) include human participants, so usability claims rest on limited evidence; the review itself stood at Major Revision at snapshot ([[../../../LargeLanguageModelsForOntologyEngineering/summary|SLR]]).
- **Structure, hierarchy, and domain/range discipline are the consistent failure modes.** Missing SubClassOf/SubPropertyOf chains, unstable property modeling, 50–73% signature compliance, and systematic domain/range-triple failures on raw file dumps mean generated fragments need review — industry fragments needing only minor edits but open-ended EU fragments needing moderate revision, with CQ specificity driving quality ([[../../../CQ4OE/summary|CQ4OE]], [[../../../FrenchLegalKnowledgeGraph/summary|FrenchLegalKG]], [[../../../OpenOntologies/summary|OpenOntologies]], [[../../../OntoExtend/summary|OntoExtend]]).
- **Wrappers and entity resolution are unsolved manual work.** Wrappers are currently manual, entity resolution is out of scope, citation queries are capped by 71% OpenCitations mapping coverage, and prose patching of the domain rider degenerates into ineffective "whack-a-mole" (concept smearing) ([[../../../NaturalLanguageKnowledgeGraphQueryExecution/summary|NLKGQ]]).
- **Benchmark quality dominates headline scores.** Non-deterministic LIMIT-without-ORDER-BY references, echo queries, and undocumented dependencies caused multi-point run-to-run variation on DBLP-QuAD 2.0; single-domain or co-evolved question sets may overstate novel-query accuracy; heuristic thresholds (0.6–0.95 bands, 0.72 retrieval floor) were tuned on development corpora; contamination from public ontologies, small public samples, single representative documents, manual wrappers, auto-generated (non-expert) SQL baselines, and local-model-only or single-model ablations all bound the claims ([[../../../NaturalLanguageKnowledgeGraphQueryExecution/summary|NLKGQ]], [[../../../OntoExtend/summary|OntoExtend]], [[../../../OpenOntologies/summary|OpenOntologies]]).
- **The prescribed direction is still aspirational.** Standardized benchmarks plus hybrid LLM-human workflows preserving logical consistency and domain fidelity are the recommended way forward, not an established practice ([[../../../LargeLanguageModelsForOntologyEngineering/summary|SLR]]).
