# Agreements — ontology_ai

Scope: only the three topic digests (`topics/*/digest.md`) and the eight source summaries they link. No wiki pages, raw sources, or web. Each section below is a claim two or more independent sources jointly support, with the converging evidence and the boundary the sources place on it. Topics 01 and 02 rest on a single summarized source each, so they contribute only where they corroborate topic 03's multi-source findings. Points the sources contest (fragmentation-as-immaturity vs coverage gaps, manual-wrapper cost, bottleneck location, expressivity choice, scaling answers, score transfer, hybrid-workflow evidence) stay in `disagreements.md` and are not repeated here.

## A complete, readable domain ontology in context enables reliable zero-shot NL-to-SPARQL

Two independent systems agree on the mechanism and its size: placing the full domain OWL ontology — declared types, domain/range, class hierarchy — plus SPARQL instructions directly in context drives single-call zero-shot query generation to 89.9% Match and 81.3% Exact on the revised 1,000-question DBLP-QuAD 3.1, 98/100 on SemOpenAlex, and 100% on 21 neuroimaging competency questions ([[../../NaturalLanguageKnowledgeGraphQueryExecution/summary|NaturalLanguageKnowledgeGraphQueryExecution]]), and readable names plus `rdfs:comment`/`rdfs:label` with explicit domain/range carry a 21-question MRI benchmark to 100% SPARQL versus 57% for SQL auto-generated from the same ontology, with an ablation spread from 100% (full Turtle) down to 5–19% (anonymised abstract forms) while prompts and temperatures matter little ([[../../NaturalLanguageAccessToDomainSpecificMetadata/summary|NaturalLanguageAccessToDomainSpecificMetadataAReusableFrameworkForLlmQueryGeneration]]). Wrapper ontologies — a clean typed namespace shown to the model, deterministically rewritten to native predicates before execution — extend the trick to opaque vocabularies, contributing ~25 Exact-match points on DBLP. Boundary: purpose-built, LLM-readable ontologies with a task harness (instructions, rider, output repair); raw heterogeneous Turtle dumped without support hurts (see `disagreements.md`), and wrappers are currently manual with entity resolution out of scope.

## LLMs draft terms fluently but collapse on structure

The vocabulary-to-structure gradient is measured independently three ways: nine LLMs recover terms at F1 ~59–67% yet fall to ~32% on properties, ~12–15% on triples/axioms, ~17% on hierarchy closure, and ~2% full axiom coverage ([[../../CQ4OE/summary|Cq4oeABenchmarkForAssessingLlmAssistedOntologyGenerationFromCompetencyQuestions]]); a 6,370-article legal pipeline reaches 100% JSON validity and ~99.98% class coverage but only 50–73% signature compliance from typing errors and novel domain–range reuse ([[../../FrenchLegalKnowledgeGraph/summary|FrenchLegalKnowledgeGraph]]); and raw OWL file reading fails systematically on domain/range triples in a 9-ontology, 3,042-axiom ablation ([[../../OpenOntologies/summary|OpenOntologiesToolAugmentedOntologyEngineeringWithStableMatchingAlignment]]). Converging evidence: unconstrained LLM output is never committable — domain variation dominates model or strategy choice. Boundary: each gradient is measured on its authors' tasks and metrics (CQ-conditioned coverage reaches ~23%, and per-CQ verification can hit 100%), so the shared claim is the shape of the collapse, not a portable F1 number.

## Grounding plus deterministic normalization plus symbolic validation is the shared fix

All six topic-03 sources converge on the same remedy package rather than any single trick: retrieved ontology slices in the prompt (FAISS per-CQ retrieval answering all 39 test CQs with <2% superfluous elements — [[../../OntoExtend/summary|OntoextendAFrameworkForRequirementDrivenAndScalableOntologyExtensionWithLlms]]; live content-conditioned slice retrieval cutting ~94% of tokens with recall ~70% → ~95% and zero false merges — [[../../OntologyGuidedDeduplicationAwareExtractionLayer/summary|AnOntologyGuidedDeduplicationAwareExtractionLayerForKnowledgeGraphConstructionFromHeterogeneousDocuments]]), controlled readable vocabularies with declared types ([[../../NaturalLanguageAccessToDomainSpecificMetadata/summary|NaturalLanguageAccessToDomainSpecificMetadataAReusableFrameworkForLlmQueryGeneration]]), deterministic normalization and deduplication (embedding fusion preserving statements while compacting; six rule-based dedup algorithms under a non-overridable hard-conflict guard; stable 1-to-1 matching lifting OAEI Anatomy alignment from F1 0.182 to 0.832 — [[../../FrenchLegalKnowledgeGraph/summary|FrenchLegalKnowledgeGraph]], [[../../OntologyGuidedDeduplicationAwareExtractionLayer/summary|AnOntologyGuidedDeduplicationAwareExtractionLayerForKnowledgeGraphConstructionFromHeterogeneousDocuments]], [[../../OpenOntologies/summary|OpenOntologiesToolAugmentedOntologyEngineeringWithStableMatchingAlignment]]), and symbolic validators with human review (SHACL/RDFLib gates, OOPS! + Pellet/HermiT consistency checks, engineer CQ-verification). Boundary: the package is validated per-system on single-domain corpora with heuristic thresholds tuned on development data — which components carry the weight varies by task (see `disagreements.md` on bottleneck location).

## Structured and tool-mediated access beats raw context dumps

Three independent ablations agree on the direction: structured MCP/SPARQL tool access reaches F1 0.717 versus 0.431 for unaided inference and 0.323 for dumping the raw OWL file into context ([[../../OpenOntologies/summary|OpenOntologiesToolAugmentedOntologyEngineeringWithStableMatchingAlignment]]); retrieval-grounded per-question extension answers all 39 test CQs with near-zero syntax errors where full-context stuffing cannot scale ([[../../OntoExtend/summary|OntoextendAFrameworkForRequirementDrivenAndScalableOntologyExtensionWithLlms]]); and live slice retrieval plus deterministic dedup lifts search recall while cutting tokens ~94% ([[../../OntologyGuidedDeduplicationAwareExtractionLayer/summary|AnOntologyGuidedDeduplicationAwareExtractionLayerForKnowledgeGraphConstructionFromHeterogeneousDocuments]]). Converging evidence: how the ontology reaches the model (retrieved slice, tool call, wrapper) matters more than how much of it is pasted. Boundary: each comparison is in-setting (alignment F1, extension verification, extraction recall) — not a universal "tools always beat context", since a small well-designed ontology in context is itself the winning setup above.

## Competency questions are the shared evaluation unit

The benchmark paper, the extension framework, and the legal pipeline independently evaluate functionally through CQs — CQ-aligned term/ontology metrics across 54 runs and 162 ontologies ([[../../CQ4OE/summary|Cq4oeABenchmarkForAssessingLlmAssistedOntologyGenerationFromCompetencyQuestions]]), per-fragment CQ verification at 100% with engineer correctness/completeness ratings ([[../../OntoExtend/summary|OntoextendAFrameworkForRequirementDrivenAndScalableOntologyExtensionWithLlms]]), and CQ-as-SPARQL tests over the constructed graph ([[../../FrenchLegalKnowledgeGraph/summary|FrenchLegalKnowledgeGraph]]) — all preferring axiom/term coverage and query-execution checks over lexical overlap. Boundary: CQ sets are mostly author-built and co-evolved with their systems (99/118 CQs, 39 test CQs, 21-question sets), with contamination risk on public ontologies — the shared claim is the evaluation shape, and the SLR's call for standardized benchmarks (topic 01) is exactly the missing piece.

## The field needs shared benchmarks; single-domain scores do not transfer unchecked

The systematic review's central prescription — standardized benchmarks plus hybrid LLM-human workflows to repair fragmentation across 30 papers / 41 studies ([[../LargeLanguageModelsForOntologyEngineering/summary|SLR]]) — is corroborated from below by the topic-03 papers' own caveats: single-domain or co-evolved question sets that may overstate novel-query accuracy, non-standard tasks and metrics across systems, heuristic thresholds tuned on development corpora, and unreleased code blocking reproduction. Converging evidence: every headline score in this research was measured on its author's home turf. Boundary: the sources agree on the prescription, not on the diagnosis — whether fragmentation reflects genuine immaturity or search-term under-coverage is contested (see `disagreements.md`).
