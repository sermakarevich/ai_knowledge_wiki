# Large Language Models for Ontology Engineering: A Systematic Literature Review | www.semantic-web-journal.net

This folder covers the Semantic Web Journal survey article by Jiayi Li, Maria Poveda, and Daniel Garijo, which systematically reviews 30 papers (41 extracted studies) on using LLMs across the ontology-engineering lifecycle. Its central diagnosis is fragmentation — unstandardized tasks, datasets, metrics, and workflows with weak reproducibility — pointing to standardized benchmarks and hybrid LLM-human workflows. The article stands at Major Revision, with two solicited reviews calling it comprehensive but asking for structural tightening and a unifying taxonomy diagram.
