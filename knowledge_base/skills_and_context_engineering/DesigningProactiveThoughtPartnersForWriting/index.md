# Designing Proactive Thought Partners for Writing

This paper proposes *proactive thought partners* — customizable AI partners that take initiative to offer higher-level cognitive support (ideation, reflection, argument development) during writing without waiting for explicit prompts. It builds a Next.js/Markdown probe where writers configure each partner by role plus proactivity (event triggers and contextual heuristics), and deploys it with 16 participants for one week: writers planned prospectively in setup, mostly ignored tags to preserve flow, and used opened suggestions for ideation and self-monitoring. It is directly relevant to anyone designing proactive AI features where timing, intrusiveness, and user control matter.
