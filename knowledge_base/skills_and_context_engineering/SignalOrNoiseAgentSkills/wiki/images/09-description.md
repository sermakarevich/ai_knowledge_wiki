**What it shows.** The page contains two side‑by‑side *code‑comparison* figures (Fig. 1 and Fig. 2), not chart plots, so there are no numeric axes. **"Axes" / structure.** Instead of x/y axes, the organizing dimensions are *condition* (C0 no‑skill vs. C1 skill) × *attempt* (1st vs. 2nd), with the dependent outcome being pass/fail of the assertion.

**Trend.** The consistent pattern is: first attempt fails for the same structural reason under both conditions; on retry the two conditions *diverge* — the unanchored model patches the symptom (renames / changes the element), whereas the skill‑anchored model applies a rule‑driven fix (here, content + explicit visibility) that resolves the assertion.

**Takeaway.** Skill content steers the *mechanism* of repair rather than guaranteeing a per‑case win: in Fig. 1 it produces a counter‑productive collision (still fails), while in Fig. 2 it produces the correct fix (passes, contributing roughly a +18 pp content effect on that pair vs. only about –1 pp at the model‑level average).
