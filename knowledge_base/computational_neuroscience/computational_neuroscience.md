# Computational Neuroscience

Research on connectomics, functional brain imaging, neural simulation, and whole-brain emulation — quantifying what it would take to model biological brains in silico, from simple organisms up to humans.

## Papers

- [[FromWormToHumanScalingBrainEmulation/summary]] — Feasibility roadmap for whole-brain emulation: real-time human emulation needs ~6e20 FLOP/s, within 10x of mid-2020s AI clusters; $5-50B, 10-25yr program.
