# Connections

- [[LimitsOfALimitlessScience/summary|The Limits of a Limitless Science]] -- same-problem-different-method: Jaki argues science's power comes from restricting itself to the quantitative (matter, measurement) and is structurally blind to quality, meaning, and purpose; this is the same structural split McGilchrist draws between the left hemisphere's abstracted, measurable "What" and the right hemisphere's contextual, qualitative "How" -- two independent lines of argument (philosophy of science vs. neuropsychology) converging on one distinction.
- [[CritiqueOfAgentModel/summary|Critique of Agent Model]] -- shares-technique: this paper's distinction between "agentic" systems (competent at externally scaffolded, script-following workflows) and "agentive" systems (genuine, endogenous goals and identity) maps closely onto McGilchrist's emissary/Master split -- a clever, narrow procedure-follower mistaking itself for the whole versus a system with real, contextually-grounded purpose; worth reading together when evaluating whether an agent harness is building genuine judgment or just a more elaborate script.
- [[RobotsNeedMoreThanVLAsAndWorldModels/summary|Robots Need More Than VLAs & World Models]] -- applies-in-practice: the paper's core bottleneck -- that raw embodied, contextual human experience (video, motion, sensor data) has no mechanism to become explicit, robot-usable supervision -- is a live engineering instance of McGilchrist's "handback" problem, where the left hemisphere's abstracted representation must be re-grounded in the right hemisphere's embodied whole rather than mistaken for it.
- [[ai_management/ZeroAlignment/summary|Zero Alignment]] -- applies-in-practice: this paper's finding that AI coding agents boost individual output while eroding team-level shared understanding is a small-scale, present-day echo of McGilchrist's Roseto argument in the Conclusion -- efficiency and speed at the individual/parts level accumulating into a loss of the broader, relational whole that made the work coherent in the first place.

_Note: this KB is weighted toward AI agent/engineering research, so these connections read McGilchrist's neuropsychological/historical argument through that lens (procedure vs. judgment, individual efficiency vs. collective coherence) rather than through parallel neuroscience or cultural-history sources, which this KB does not yet hold._
